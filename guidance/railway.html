Railway Exam Guidance

Complete guidance for major RRB and railway recruitment examinations, including eligibility, subjects, books, selection process and preparation.

What are Railway Exams?

Railway recruitment examinations are competitive examinations conducted mainly through Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) and Railway Recruitment Cells (RRCs) for different technical, non-technical, operational and Level-1 posts in Indian Railways. Eligibility, syllabus, age, medical standards and selection stages vary according to the post and recruitment notification.

Major Railway Examinations

01

RRB NTPC

Recruitment for Non-Technical Popular Categories such as Commercial cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk cum Typist, Junior Clerk cum Typist, Trains Clerk and graduate-level NTPC posts, as notified.

02

RRB Group D / Level-1

Recruitment for Level-1 posts in Indian Railways. Eligibility and posts are determined by the applicable Centralised Employment Notice.

03

RRB ALP

Assistant Loco Pilot recruitment. Technical qualification and prescribed medical standards are important for this post.

04

RRB Technician

Recruitment for Technician posts in different technical grades and categories as specified in the recruitment notification.

05

RRB JE

Junior Engineer recruitment for eligible engineering and diploma candidates in notified technical disciplines.

06

RRB Paramedical

Recruitment for notified healthcare and paramedical posts such as Nursing Superintendent, Pharmacist and other categories.

07

RPF Constable

Recruitment for Constable posts in the Railway Protection Force, subject to the applicable recruitment notification.

08

RPF Sub-Inspector

Recruitment for Sub-Inspector posts in the Railway Protection Force, subject to the applicable notification.

09

Other Railway Recruitments

Railway departments may conduct other recruitment through RRBs, RRCs or other authorised recruitment processes for specific posts.

Railway Exams by Educational Qualification

Level Major Examinations / Posts Typical Qualification
10th / Level-1 RRB Group D / Level-1 10th pass or ITI / equivalent qualification, as prescribed in the relevant notification.
12th / Undergraduate RRB NTPC undergraduate posts 12th (+2) or equivalent qualification, as prescribed for the post.
Graduation RRB NTPC graduate posts, RPF SI and other graduate-level posts Bachelor's degree or the qualification specified in the notification.
Diploma / Engineering RRB JE, ALP, Technician and other technical posts Relevant diploma, degree, ITI or technical qualification depending on the post.

The exact qualification is always post-specific. Read the relevant CEN before applying.

Common Railway Exam Subjects

➗

Mathematics

Number System, BODMAS, decimals, fractions, LCM, HCF, ratio, percentage, profit and loss, time and work, time and distance, simple and compound interest, algebra, geometry, mensuration and related topics.

🧠

General Intelligence & Reasoning

Analogies, series, coding-decoding, mathematical operations, relationships, syllogism, Venn diagrams, classification, directions, analytical reasoning and other applicable topics.

🔬

General Science

Physics, Chemistry and Life Sciences at the level prescribed for the particular examination. RRB syllabi commonly refer to school-level science for relevant posts.

🌍

General Awareness

Current affairs, science and technology, sports, culture, personalities, economics, politics and other important national and international developments.

💻

Computer Awareness

Computer basics, hardware, software, operating systems, internet and related topics where included in the applicable examination.

⚙️

Technical Subjects

Technical questions are included in examinations such as ALP, Technician and JE according to the relevant trade, discipline and notification.

Railway Exam Selection Process

01

Online Application

Apply online through the official RRB/RRC recruitment portal specified in the notification.

02

CBT / Written Examination

Computer Based Test stages depend on the recruitment.

03

Skill / Aptitude / Physical Test

Applicable according to the post, such as typing, aptitude, physical efficiency or other prescribed tests.

04

Document Verification

Original educational, identity and category documents are verified.

05

Medical Examination

Medical fitness is required according to the post and prescribed railway medical standards.

06

Final Appointment

Appointment is subject to merit, vacancies, document verification, medical fitness and applicable rules.

Railway Exam Eligibility

Educational Qualification

Educational qualification depends on the post. Railway recruitment may include 10th-pass, ITI, 12th, graduation, diploma and engineering qualifications.

Age Limit

Age limits vary by recruitment and post. Category-wise age relaxation is provided according to the applicable rules and notification.

Nationality

Nationality and other citizenship conditions are specified in the relevant recruitment notification.

Medical Standards

Several railway posts have specific medical standards. Candidates should check the medical requirement of the post before applying, especially for safety-related and technical posts.

Documents Required

  • Valid photo identity proof
  • 10th / 12th / ITI / Diploma / Degree certificates and marksheets, as applicable
  • Recent photograph and signature as required
  • SC / ST / OBC-NCL / EWS / PwBD certificate, if applicable
  • Relevant technical certificate or professional qualification, where applicable
  • Other certificates specifically required by the recruitment notification

NCERT for Railway Exams

NCERT is not compulsory for every railway examination. It is particularly useful for building basic Science concepts and can also help with foundational General Knowledge. For technical posts, candidates should give priority to the qualification-specific technical syllabus and previous-year questions.

Preparation Strategy

📚

Build Basics

Complete Mathematics, Reasoning and General Science fundamentals before moving to advanced practice.

📰

Current Affairs

Revise recent national, international, sports, science and technology and other important events.

📝

PYQs

Solve previous-year questions to understand the level, repeated concepts and question style.

⏱️

Mock Tests

Take timed mock tests and analyse incorrect and skipped questions.

⚙️

Technical Preparation

For technical posts, follow the exact trade or discipline syllabus mentioned in the notification.

🏃

Physical / Aptitude Preparation

Where applicable, prepare for PET, aptitude or skill tests well before the relevant stage.

Important Railway Exam Information

❌

Negative Marking

Negative marking is not identical for every railway recruitment. For example, the RRB ALP CEN 01/2024 specifies 1/3 mark deduction for a wrong answer in CBT-1 and CBT-2. Always check the current CEN.

🩺

Medical Test

Medical requirements can be important for railway posts and vary according to the post and medical category.

⌨️

Typing / Skill Test

Some NTPC posts require a Typing Skill Test. Other recruitments may have different skill or aptitude tests.

🏃

PET

Physical Efficiency Test requirements apply to recruitment where prescribed, such as relevant Level-1 or RPF recruitment stages.

📊

Normalization

When CBT is held in multiple shifts, the recruitment notification may provide for normalization of marks.

📅

Notification

Vacancies, dates, fees, qualification, age, syllabus and selection stages can change. Always use the latest official CEN.

Important Note

Railway recruitment is not one single examination. RRB NTPC, Level-1/Group D, ALP, Technician, JE, Paramedical, RPF and other recruitments have different eligibility conditions, subjects, exam stages, medical standards and selection rules. Candidates should read the latest official Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) before applying.

रेलवे परीक्षाएँ क्या हैं?

रेलवे भर्ती परीक्षाएँ मुख्य रूप से Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) और Railway Recruitment Cells (RRCs) के माध्यम से भारतीय रेलवे के विभिन्न technical, non-technical, operational और Level-1 पदों के लिए आयोजित प्रतियोगी परीक्षाएँ हैं। पद के अनुसार eligibility, syllabus, age, medical standards और selection stages अलग-अलग हो सकते हैं।

RRB NTPC

Commercial cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk cum Typist, Junior Clerk cum Typist, Trains Clerk तथा graduate-level NTPC posts की भर्ती, जैसा notification में निर्धारित हो।

भारतीय रेलवे के Level-1 पदों के लिए भर्ती। Posts और eligibility संबंधित CEN में निर्धारित होती है।

03

RRB ALP

Assistant Loco Pilot भर्ती। Technical qualification और निर्धारित medical standards महत्वपूर्ण हैं।

04

RRB Technician

विभिन्न technical grades और categories में Technician पदों के लिए भर्ती।

05

RRB JE

Junior Engineer पदों के लिए engineering और diploma योग्य candidates की भर्ती।

06

RRB Paramedical

Nursing Superintendent, Pharmacist और अन्य notified paramedical posts के लिए भर्ती।

07

RPF Constable

Railway Protection Force में Constable पदों के लिए भर्ती।

08

RPF Sub-Inspector

Railway Protection Force में Sub-Inspector पदों के लिए भर्ती।

09

अन्य रेलवे भर्तियाँ

विशिष्ट पदों के लिए RRB, RRC या अन्य अधिकृत भर्ती प्रक्रियाओं के माध्यम से भर्ती हो सकती है।
