State PSC Exam Guidance

Complete guidance for State PCS and Public Service Commission examinations, including Prelims, Mains, subjects, books, eligibility, interview and state-specific preparation.

What is State PSC?

State Public Service Commissions conduct recruitment examinations for various state government services. Major State Civil Services examinations are commonly known as State PCS or State Civil Services examinations. The exact exam pattern, subjects, optional papers, language papers, eligibility and state-specific syllabus vary from one State PSC to another.

Major State PSC Examinations

01

UPPSC PCS

Uttar Pradesh Combined State / Upper Subordinate Services examination for various state services.

02

BPSC

Bihar Combined Competitive Examination for various Bihar state services.

03

MPPSC

Madhya Pradesh State Service Examination for notified state services.

04

RPSC RAS

Rajasthan State and Subordinate Services Combined Competitive Examination.

05

MPSC Rajyaseva

Maharashtra Civil Services Gazetted Group A and Group B examination and related state service recruitment.

06

HPSC HCS

Haryana Civil Services and allied services examination.

07

UKPSC PCS

Uttarakhand Combined State Civil / Upper Subordinate Services recruitment.

08

JPSC Civil Services

Jharkhand Combined Civil Services Examination.

09

OPSC / WBPSC / GPSC / CGPSC

Odisha, West Bengal, Gujarat, Chhattisgarh and other State PSCs conduct their respective state service examinations.

State PSC Exam Structure

Stage Purpose Common Components
Preliminary Examination Screening / shortlisting for Mains General Studies, General Aptitude/CSAT or state-specific papers, depending on the PSC
Main Examination Detailed assessment and merit Essay, General Studies, language papers, optional/state-specific papers depending on the commission
Interview / Personality Test Assessment of personality, suitability and knowledge State, current affairs, educational background and service-related awareness

Do not assume that every State PSC follows the UPSC pattern. For example, Rajasthan's official 2026 syllabus separately publishes Prelims and Mains schemes, while Maharashtra PSC publishes its own examination schemes and syllabus.

Eligibility

Requirement General Information
Educational Qualification Graduation or the qualification specified by the concerned State PSC is commonly required for State Civil Services.
Age Limit Varies by state, service, category and notification.
Nationality Generally Indian citizenship requirements apply, subject to the relevant notification.
Language Some State PSCs prescribe state-language knowledge or language papers.
Other Conditions Specific services may have additional physical, professional or educational requirements.

Selection Process

01

Notification

Read the latest State PSC notification, syllabus, vacancies and eligibility.

02

Prelims

Objective screening examination according to the commission's scheme.

03

Mains

Descriptive examination for shortlisted candidates.

04

Interview

Personality test / interview where prescribed.

05

Document Verification

Verification of educational, identity and category documents.

06

Final Merit

Final selection according to the applicable scheme, merit and service rules.

Documents Required

  • Valid photo identity proof
  • 10th / 12th certificates and marksheets
  • Graduation degree and marksheets
  • Category certificate, if applicable
  • Domicile / residence certificate, where required
  • Recent photograph and signature
  • Other documents specified in the current notification

Important Note

State PSC examinations are not identical to UPSC. The exact Prelims, Mains, optional papers, language papers, negative marking, eligibility and state-specific syllabus can change by commission and notification. Use the latest official notification and syllabus as the final authority.

State PSC क्या है?

State Public Service Commissions राज्य सरकार की विभिन्न सेवाओं में भर्ती के लिए examinations आयोजित करते हैं। State Civil Services को कई जगह State PCS के नाम से जाना जाता है। अलग-अलग राज्यों में exam pattern, subjects, optional papers, language papers, eligibility और state-specific syllabus अलग हो सकते हैं।

बिहार की विभिन्न state services के लिए Combined Competitive Examination।

07

Odisha, West Bengal, Gujarat, Chhattisgarh और अन्य State PSCs अपने state service examinations आयोजित करते हैं।

State PSC Exam Structure

Stage Purpose Common Components
Prelims Mains के लिए screening / shortlisting General Studies, General Aptitude/CSAT या state-specific papers
Mains Detailed assessment और merit Essay, General Studies, language papers, optional/state-specific papers

हर State PSC को UPSC के समान नहीं मानना चाहिए। Rajasthan और Maharashtra जैसे commissions अपने अलग examination schemes और syllabi प्रकाशित करते हैं।.
