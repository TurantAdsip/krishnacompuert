Police Exam Guidance

Complete guidance for major Central and State Police recruitment examinations, including subjects, books, eligibility, physical tests, selection process and preparation.

What are Police Exams?

Police recruitment examinations are conducted by central and state recruitment authorities for posts such as Constable, Head Constable, Sub-Inspector and other police or uniformed services. The exact eligibility, subjects, physical standards, medical requirements and selection stages depend on the recruiting authority and post.

Major Police Examinations

01

SSC GD Constable

SSC recruitment for Constable (GD) in CAPFs and SSF and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles, as notified.

02

SSC CPO

SSC Sub-Inspector recruitment in Delhi Police and CAPFs.

03

Delhi Police Constable

Recruitment for Constable (Executive) posts in Delhi Police through the process specified by SSC.

04

Delhi Police Head Constable

Head Constable recruitment for notified categories such as Ministerial or AWO/TPO, subject to the applicable notice.

05

State Police Constable

State-level recruitment for Constable and equivalent posts. Eligibility and syllabus vary by state.

06

State Police Sub-Inspector

State-level SI recruitment. Written examination, physical stages and other requirements depend on the state.

07

Special Police / Armed Police

Recruitment for state armed police, PAC, battalions and other notified uniformed posts.

08

Jail / Prison Warder

Prison department recruitment is conducted by the relevant state authority and may have separate written and physical requirements.

09

Other Police Posts

Driver, radio operator, technical, clerical, forensic and other specialist police posts may be notified separately.

Police Exams by Educational Qualification

Qualification Major Examples Typical Requirement
10th Some State Constable / equivalent recruitments 10th pass where the relevant notification permits it.
12th SSC GD, many State Constable posts, Delhi Police Constable and selected Head Constable entries 10+2 or equivalent, depending on the post.
Graduation SSC CPO, many State SI recruitments Bachelor's degree or the qualification prescribed for the post.
Technical / Diploma Police technical, radio, communications and specialist posts Relevant technical qualification as notified.

There is no single educational qualification for all police jobs. Always check the current recruitment notification.

Exam-wise Subjects

Exam / Recruitment Subjects
SSC GD Constable General Intelligence & Reasoning; General Knowledge & General Awareness; Elementary Mathematics; English/Hindi
SSC CPO — SI Paper-I: General Intelligence & Reasoning; General Knowledge & General Awareness; Quantitative Aptitude; English Comprehension. Paper-II: English Language & Comprehension
Delhi Police Constable (Executive) General Awareness; Quantitative Aptitude; General Intelligence; English Language
Delhi Police Head Constable (Ministerial) General Awareness; Quantitative Aptitude; General Intelligence; English Language; Computer Fundamentals
State Police Constable Common areas may include General Knowledge/Current Affairs, General Science, Mathematics/Arithmetic, Reasoning and state-specific General Knowledge; exact syllabus varies by state.
State Police SI Common areas may include General Studies/General Knowledge, Current Affairs, Reasoning, Mathematics/Arithmetic, Language and state-specific knowledge; exact syllabus varies by state.
Armed Police / PAC Usually the state-notified Constable/SI syllabus plus physical requirements; exact subjects depend on the recruitment.
Jail / Prison Warder Usually General Knowledge, Current Affairs, Reasoning, Mathematics/Language and state-specific topics; exact syllabus varies by state.

State Police examinations cannot be given one universal subject list. The state recruitment board's latest syllabus must be followed.

NCERT in Police Exams

NCERT is not compulsory for every police examination. It is useful mainly for basic General Science and school-level concepts. For State Police exams, state-specific GK and the official syllabus are often more important.

Physical Tests

🏃

Running

Many police recruitments include a running or endurance test. Distance, time and qualifying standards depend on the recruitment.

📏

Physical Standards

Height, chest and other standards may apply depending on post, gender, category and recruitment rules.

💪

Strength / Events

Some recruitments may prescribe long jump, high jump or other physical events.

🩺

Medical

Medical fitness is assessed according to the service and recruitment rules.

Physical standards are not universal. Candidates must follow the exact PST/PET requirements in the relevant notification.

Selection Process

01

Online Application

Apply through the official recruitment authority's website.

02

Written / CBT

Computer Based Test or written examination as prescribed.

03

PST / PET

Physical Standard Test and/or Physical Efficiency Test where applicable.

04

Skill / Trade Test

Applicable for driver, technical, clerical or specialist posts.

05

Document Verification

Original educational, identity and category documents are verified.

06

Medical & Final Merit

Medical fitness and final merit are determined according to the applicable rules.

Eligibility

Educational Qualification

Police posts may require 10th, 12th, graduation or a technical qualification depending on the post.

Age Limit

Age limits and category-wise relaxations vary by examination and recruiting authority.

Nationality

Nationality and citizenship requirements are specified in the applicable recruitment notification.

Physical & Medical

Police recruitment may include mandatory physical and medical standards. These standards vary considerably between central and state recruitments.

Documents Required

  • Valid photo identity proof
  • 10th / 12th / Degree / Diploma / ITI certificates and marksheets, as applicable
  • Recent photograph and signature
  • SC / ST / OBC / EWS / PwBD certificate, if applicable
  • Domicile / residence certificate where required by the state recruitment
  • NCC / sports / driving licence / technical certificates, where applicable
  • Other documents listed in the latest notification

Preparation Strategy

📚

Written Preparation

Complete the exact syllabus and strengthen GK, Current Affairs, Maths and Reasoning.

🗺️

State GK

For State Police exams, study state history, geography, culture, polity and current affairs as prescribed.

📝

PYQs

Solve previous-year papers to understand difficulty and repeated topics.

⏱️

Mock Tests

Take timed tests and analyse mistakes to improve speed and accuracy.

🏃

Physical Training

Start running and fitness training early rather than waiting for the written result.

🩺

Medical Awareness

Check the medical standards of your target post and follow healthy fitness habits.

Important Police Exam Information

🇮🇳

Central Police

SSC conducts examinations such as Constable (GD) in CAPFs/SSF and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles, and SI in Delhi Police/CAPFs.

🏛️

State Police

State recruitment boards conduct their own Constable, SI and other recruitments with state-specific rules.

❌

Negative Marking

Negative marking varies by examination. Check the current notification rather than assuming one rule for all police exams.

📅

Notification

Vacancies, age, fees, syllabus, physical standards and selection stages can change from one recruitment to another.

Important Note

Police recruitment is not one single examination. SSC GD, SSC CPO, Delhi Police, UP Police and other State Police recruitments have different eligibility, subjects, physical standards, medical rules and selection processes. Always read the latest official notification and syllabus before applying.

Police परीक्षाएँ क्या हैं?

Police recruitment examinations के माध्यम से Constable, Head Constable, Sub-Inspector और अन्य police या uniformed service posts पर भर्ती की जाती है। Central और State Police की eligibility, subjects, physical standards, medical requirements और selection process अलग-अलग हो सकते हैं।

01

SSC GD Constable

02

SSC CPO

Delhi Police और CAPFs में Sub-Inspector भर्ती।

03

Delhi Police Constable

Delhi Police में Constable (Executive) की भर्ती।

Applicable notification के अनुसार Head Constable की विभिन्न categories में भर्ती।

05

06

State Police Sub-Inspector

राज्य स्तर पर SI की भर्ती; written, physical और अन्य requirements राज्य के अनुसार अलग होती हैं।

07

Armed Police / PAC

State Armed Police, PAC, battalions और अन्य uniformed posts की भर्ती।

08

Jail / Prison Warder

Prison department में Warder और related posts की भर्ती।

09

अन्य Police Posts

Driver, radio operator, technical, clerical, forensic और specialist police posts की भर्ती अलग से हो सकती है।
