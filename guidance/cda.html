What is CDS?

The Combined Defence Services (CDS) Examination is conducted by UPSC for admission to officer-training academies of the Indian Armed Forces. The main entries covered through CDS are the Indian Military Academy (IMA), Indian Naval Academy (INA), Air Force Academy (AFA) and Officers' Training Academy (OTA), subject to the applicable notification.

UPSC conducts CDS twice a year. The UPSC 2026 examination archive lists CDS (I), 2026 and CDS (II), 2026; CDS (II), 2026 was notified on 20 May 2026 and is scheduled for 13 September 2026. citeturn0search0turn0search1

CDS Entries / Academies

01

IMA

Indian Military Academy — Permanent Commission entry for eligible male candidates as specified in the notification.

02

INA

Indian Naval Academy — Permanent Commission entry for eligible candidates with the required educational qualification.

03

AFA

Air Force Academy — Flying/other applicable entry as specified in the notification.

04

OTA

Officers' Training Academy — Short Service Commission entry for eligible candidates.

CDS Eligibility

Academy Educational Qualification
IMA Degree from a recognised university or equivalent.
INA Degree in Engineering from a recognised university/institution.
AFA Degree from a recognised university with Physics and Mathematics at 10+2 level, or Bachelor of Engineering, subject to notification conditions.
OTA Degree from a recognised university or equivalent.

Candidates appearing in the final year/semester of the qualifying degree may be eligible subject to the conditions in the relevant UPSC notification. Exact age, gender, marital-status and other conditions vary by academy and notification. citeturn0search19

CDS Written Exam Pattern

Academy Paper Marks Duration
IMA English + General Knowledge + Elementary Mathematics 300 6 Hours total
INA English + General Knowledge + Elementary Mathematics 300 6 Hours total
AFA English + General Knowledge + Elementary Mathematics 300 6 Hours total
OTA English + General Knowledge 200 4 Hours total

For IMA, INA and AFA, each of the three papers carries 100 marks and has a 2-hour duration. For OTA, English and General Knowledge are each 100 marks with 2 hours per paper. citeturn0search19

CDS Subjects

Subject What to Study
English Grammar, vocabulary, sentence usage, comprehension, synonyms, antonyms and basic language skills
General Knowledge Current affairs, History, Geography, Polity, Economy, General Science and defence-related awareness
Elementary Mathematics Arithmetic, Algebra, Trigonometry, Geometry, Mensuration and Statistics

OTA candidates do not have the Elementary Mathematics paper in the written examination. UPSC's official previous-paper archive lists English, General Knowledge and Elementary Mathematics papers for CDS. citeturn0search2

Selection Process

01

Written Exam

UPSC written examination according to the selected academy.

02

SSB Interview

Successful written candidates are called for the Services Selection Board process as applicable.

03

SSB Assessment

Intelligence/personality assessment, group tasks and interview as prescribed.

04

Medical

Prescribed service medical examination.

05

Final Merit

Final selection depends on applicable written/SSB performance, vacancies and eligibility.

06

Training

Selected candidates join the relevant academy for training.

CDS क्या है?

Combined Defence Services (CDS) Examination का आयोजन UPSC द्वारा Indian Military Academy (IMA), Indian Naval Academy (INA), Air Force Academy (AFA) और Officers' Training Academy (OTA) में officer training के लिए admission हेतु किया जाता है।

UPSC CDS examination साल में दो बार आयोजित करता है। 2026 में CDS (I) और CDS (II) दोनों examinations listed हैं। CDS (II), 2026 की notification 20 May 2026 को जारी हुई और परीक्षा 13 September 2026 के लिए निर्धारित है। citeturn0search0turn0search1

CDS Entries / Academies

01

IMA

Indian Military Academy — notification के अनुसार eligible candidates के लिए Permanent Commission entry।

02

INA

Indian Naval Academy — required educational qualification वाले eligible candidates के लिए entry।

03

AFA

Air Force Academy — notification में दी गई applicable entry के अनुसार।

04

OTA

Officers' Training Academy — Short Service Commission entry।

CDS Eligibility

Academy Educational Qualification
IMA Recognised university से Degree या equivalent qualification।
INA Recognised university/institution से Engineering Degree।
AFA Recognised university से Degree जिसमें 10+2 level पर Physics और Mathematics हो, या Bachelor of Engineering; notification की conditions लागू होंगी।
OTA Recognised university से Degree या equivalent qualification।

Final-year/semester candidates भी notification की conditions के अनुसार eligible हो सकते हैं। Exact age, gender, marital status और अन्य conditions latest UPSC notification से check करें। citeturn0search19
