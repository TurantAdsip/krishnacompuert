What is CTET?

The Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) is conducted by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE). It is a teacher eligibility test for candidates seeking eligibility for teaching at the elementary level, subject to the applicable recruitment rules and minimum qualifications.

CTET is not itself a recruitment examination. Qualifying CTET does not by itself confer a right to appointment; final eligibility is verified by the concerned recruiting or appointing authority. citeturn0search0turn0search3

CTET Papers

01

Paper I

For candidates intending to teach Classes I to V (Primary Stage).

02

Paper II

For candidates intending to teach Classes VI to VIII (Elementary Stage).

03

Both Papers

Candidates intending to teach Classes I to VIII can appear for both papers, subject to the applicable eligibility requirements.

Eligibility

Requirement Information
Minimum Qualification Depends on the teaching level and applicable NCTE/Recruitment Rules. Candidates should check the latest CTET bulletin and the recruitment rules of the concerned school/authority.
Paper I Eligibility is linked to the qualifications prescribed for teaching at the primary stage.
Paper II Eligibility is linked to the qualifications prescribed for teaching at the elementary stage.
Final Verification Appearing in CTET does not mean eligibility has been finally verified. The recruiting/appointing authority performs final verification. citeturn0search3

CTET Selection / Recruitment Process

01

Notification

Read the latest CTET information bulletin and eligibility requirements.

02

Online Application

Complete the application form, upload required details and pay the applicable fee.

03

CTET Examination

Appear for Paper I, Paper II or both as applicable.

04

Result

Check the CTET result and qualifying status through the official website.

05

Certificate

Qualified candidates can access the certificate/marksheet through the prescribed system.

06

Teacher Recruitment

Apply separately for teacher recruitment when a school/recruiting authority issues a vacancy notification.

Important: CTET qualification itself is not a job appointment. Recruitment is conducted separately by the concerned authority according to its rules. citeturn0search3

Documents Required

  • Valid photo identity details
  • 10th certificate / marksheet details
  • Educational qualification certificates and marksheets
  • Teacher education qualification documents, wherever applicable
  • Category certificate, if applicable
  • PwD / other applicable certificates, if applicable
  • Recent photograph and signature as required by the application process
  • Other documents/details specified in the latest CTET Information Bulletin

Preparation Strategy

📚

NCERT First

Build the subject foundation from relevant NCERT textbooks before moving to exam guides.

🧠

Pedagogy

Give special attention to Child Development and Pedagogy and subject-specific pedagogy.

📝

PYQs

Solve official previous-year question papers to understand question style and recurring areas.

⏱️

Mock Tests

📖

Language Practice

Practise comprehension, grammar where relevant and language pedagogy regularly.

🎯

Revision

Maintain short notes for pedagogy, formulas, concepts and frequently tested topics.

CTET क्या है?

Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) का आयोजन Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) द्वारा किया जाता है। यह शिक्षक पात्रता परीक्षा है, जिसका उद्देश्य elementary level पर teaching eligibility से संबंधित मानदंडों को पूरा करने वाले candidates को qualify करने का अवसर देना है।

CTET स्वयं कोई recruitment examination नहीं है। CTET qualify करने से अपने आप नौकरी का अधिकार नहीं मिलता। Final eligibility संबंधित recruiting/appointing authority द्वारा verify की जाती है। citeturn0search0turn0search3
