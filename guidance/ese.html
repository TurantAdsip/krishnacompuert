Complete ESE/IES guidance — eligibility, engineering branches, subjects, books, exam pattern, selection process, interview and preparation strategy.

What is ESE?

The Engineering Services Examination (ESE), commonly called IES, is conducted by UPSC for recruitment to engineering services/posts under the Government of India.

The examination is conducted in four engineering disciplines: Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering. UPSC's 2026 examination archive confirms the ESE Preliminary Examination was notified on 26 September 2025 and held on 8 February 2026. citeturn0search2

04

Electronics & Telecommunication

Networks, signals, electronics, communication, control and related engineering areas.

Eligibility

Requirement General Information
Educational Qualification Engineering degree or other qualification accepted under the applicable ESE rules/notification.
Engineering Discipline Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, or Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering, as applicable to the examination.
Age The exact age limit and cut-off date are specified in the latest UPSC ESE notification.
Nationality Nationality/citizenship conditions are specified in the applicable rules.
Medical Standards Selected candidates must satisfy the prescribed medical and service-specific standards.

Eligibility conditions can change. Always verify the latest UPSC notification and Engineering Services Examination Rules before applying.

Selection Process

01

Preliminary Exam

General Studies & Engineering Aptitude plus the chosen engineering discipline.

02

Main Exam

Two conventional papers in the selected engineering discipline.

03

Personality Test

Interview/personality assessment for candidates who qualify the written stages.

04

Document Verification

Original documents are checked as required by UPSC.

05

Medical

Applicable medical and service standards must be satisfied.

06

Final Selection

Selection is based on the applicable rules, merit, vacancies and eligibility.

Preparation Strategy

📚

Build Core Concepts

Study every technical subject from standard engineering textbooks.

📝

Solve PYQs

Analyse official ESE papers topic-wise and identify repeated concepts.

🧮

Numerical Practice

Practise calculations regularly and maintain an error notebook.

⏱️

Mock Tests

Take full-length tests with the actual time limits and analyse mistakes.

📰

GS & Current Affairs

Prepare engineering-related current issues, environment, technology and aptitude.

🎯

Mains Answer Writing

Practise clear, step-by-step conventional answers with diagrams wherever appropriate.

ESE क्या है?

Engineering Services Examination (ESE), जिसे कई बार IES भी कहा जाता है, UPSC द्वारा आयोजित engineering examination है। इसके माध्यम से भारत सरकार की विभिन्न engineering services/posts के लिए भर्ती की जाती है।

ESE चार मुख्य engineering disciplines में होता है: Civil, Mechanical, Electrical और Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering। 2026 में ESE Preliminary परीक्षा 8 February 2026 को आयोजित हुई थी। citeturn0search2
