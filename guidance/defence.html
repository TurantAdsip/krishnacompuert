Defence Exam Guidance

Complete guidance for major Indian Defence examinations and recruitment entries, including subjects, books, eligibility, physical standards, selection process and preparation.

What are Defence Exams?

Defence recruitment and entrance examinations provide entry into the Indian Army, Indian Navy, Indian Air Force and related defence organisations. Different entries have different educational qualifications, age limits, written subjects, physical standards, medical requirements and selection stages.

Major Defence Examinations

01

NDA & NA

UPSC examination for admission to the National Defence Academy and Naval Academy. Entry is mainly for candidates after 12th.

02

CDS

UPSC Combined Defence Services examination for graduate-level entry to IMA, INA, Air Force Academy and OTA.

03

AFCAT

Indian Air Force officer-entry examination for eligible graduate candidates in notified branches.

04

Indian Army Agniveer

Recruitment for notified Agniveer categories such as General Duty, Technical, Clerk/Store Keeper Technical and other notified categories.

05

Indian Navy Agniveer SSR

10+2-based Navy entry with prescribed Mathematics and Physics requirements and other eligibility conditions.

06

Indian Navy Agniveer MR

Matriculation-based Navy entry for notified categories such as Chef, Steward and Hygienist, subject to the notification.

07

Indian Air Force Agniveervayu

Agniveervayu recruitment for eligible candidates under the current Air Force recruitment notification.

08

Coast Guard

Indian Coast Guard recruitment includes different entries and posts such as Navik and Yantrik, according to notification.

09

Other Defence Entries

Technical, medical, law, sports, NCC and other specialised defence entries may be notified separately.

NCERT in Defence Exams

NCERT is not compulsory for every Defence examination. It is especially useful for building basic Science, History, Geography and Physics/Chemistry concepts. For CDS, NDA, AFCAT and recruitment-specific exams, combine NCERT with the exact syllabus, standard preparation books and previous-year papers.

Selection Process

01

Online Application

Apply through the official portal specified for the particular entry.

02

Written / Computer Based Exam

Written examination or CBT according to the entry.

03

Physical / Skill / Aptitude

Applicable physical, skill, aptitude or fitness tests are conducted according to the entry.

04

SSB / Interview

Officer entries such as NDA and CDS involve the Services Selection Board process after the written stage.

05

Medical Examination

Medical fitness is assessed according to the applicable service and entry standards.

06

Merit & Joining

Final selection depends on merit, vacancies, medical fitness and the rules of the particular entry.

Officer Entry vs Agniveer Entry

Feature Officer Entries Agniveer / Other Recruitment
Examples NDA, CDS, AFCAT and other officer entries Army Agniveer, Navy Agniveer, Air Force Agniveervayu and other notified entries
Qualification Usually 12th or graduation depending on entry 10th, 12th, ITI or diploma depending on category
Selection Written exam + SSB/AFSB + medical, where applicable CBT/written + physical/skill + medical, depending on entry
Career Structure Commissioned officer career according to the entry Service terms and retention rules depend on the applicable Agnipath/recruitment scheme

Physical & Medical Preparation

  • Build running endurance gradually and train consistently.
  • Include strength exercises such as push-ups, squats and core exercises according to the applicable test.
  • Maintain healthy body weight and general fitness.
  • Prepare according to the exact physical test and standards in the latest notification.
  • Medical standards differ by service, branch and entry; check the official notification before applying.

Defence परीक्षाएँ क्या हैं?

Defence recruitment और entrance examinations के माध्यम से Indian Army, Indian Navy, Indian Air Force और संबंधित defence organisations में अलग-अलग entries मिलती हैं। हर entry की educational qualification, age limit, subjects, physical standards, medical requirements और selection process अलग हो सकती है।

प्रमुख Defence परीक्षाएँ

01

NDA & NA

UPSC की परीक्षा, जिसके माध्यम से National Defence Academy और Naval Academy में admission होता है।

02

CDS

Graduation के बाद IMA, INA, Air Force Academy और OTA जैसी officer entries के लिए UPSC examination।

03

AFCAT

Indian Air Force में eligible graduate candidates के लिए officer-entry examination।

04

Indian Army Agniveer

General Duty, Technical, Clerk/Store Keeper Technical और अन्य notified categories में भर्ती।

05

Navy Agniveer SSR

10+2 आधारित Navy entry, जिसमें निर्धारित Mathematics और Physics qualification आवश्यक होती है।

06

Navy Agniveer MR

Matriculation आधारित Navy entry, जिसमें notified categories शामिल होती हैं।

07

Air Force Agniveervayu

Current Air Force recruitment notification के अनुसार eligible candidates के लिए Agniveervayu भर्ती।

08

Coast Guard

Navik, Yantrik तथा अन्य notified entries के लिए भर्ती।

09

अन्य Defence Entries

Technical, medical, law, sports, NCC और specialised entries अलग-अलग notifications के माध्यम से आ सकती हैं।
