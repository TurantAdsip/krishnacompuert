Complete ESE/IES guidance — eligibility, engineering branches, subjects, books, exam pattern, selection process, interview and preparation strategy.

What is ESE?

The Engineering Services Examination (ESE), commonly called IES, is conducted by UPSC for recruitment to engineering services/posts under the Government of India.

The examination is conducted in four engineering disciplines: Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering. UPSC's 2026 examination archive confirms the ESE Preliminary Examination was notified on 26 September 2025 and held on 8 February 2026.

Engineering Branches

Infrastructure, construction, structures, transportation, water resources and related engineering.

Circuits, machines, power systems, measurements, control and related areas.

Networks, signals, electronics, communication, control and related engineering areas.

Eligibility conditions can change. Always verify the latest UPSC notification and Engineering Services Examination Rules before applying.

Selection Process

01

Preliminary Exam

General Studies & Engineering Aptitude plus the chosen engineering discipline.

02

Main Exam

Two conventional papers in the selected engineering discipline.

03

Personality Test

Interview/personality assessment for candidates who qualify the written stages.

04

Document Verification

Original documents are checked as required by UPSC.

05

Medical

Applicable medical and service standards must be satisfied.

06

Final Selection

Selection is based on the applicable rules, merit, vacancies and eligibility.

ESE क्या है?

Engineering Services Examination (ESE), जिसे सामान्यतः IES कहा जाता है, भारत सरकार के अंतर्गत engineering services/posts में भर्ती के लिए UPSC द्वारा आयोजित की जाती है।

यह परीक्षा चार engineering disciplines में आयोजित की जाती है: Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering तथा Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering। UPSC के 2026 examination archive के अनुसार ESE Preliminary Examination की notification 26 September 2025 को जारी हुई थी और परीक्षा 8 February 2026 को आयोजित हुई थी।

Engineering Branches

01

Civil Engineering

Infrastructure, construction, structures, transportation, water resources और संबंधित engineering क्षेत्रों से जुड़ी शाखा।

02

Mechanical Engineering

Thermodynamics, machines, manufacturing, industrial engineering और संबंधित क्षेत्रों से जुड़ी शाखा।

03

Electrical Engineering

Circuits, machines, power systems, measurements, control और संबंधित क्षेत्रों से जुड़ी शाखा।

04

Electronics & Telecommunication

Networks, signals, electronics, communication, control और संबंधित engineering क्षेत्रों से जुड़ी शाखा।
