What is SSC CPO?

The Staff Selection Commission Central Police Organization (SSC CPO) examination is conducted by the Staff Selection Commission for recruitment to Sub-Inspector posts in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs).

The examination provides an opportunity for eligible candidates to join police and central armed police organisations as Sub-Inspectors. The recruitment process generally includes a Computer Based Examination, Physical Standard Test/Physical Endurance Test, Paper-II, medical examination and document verification, as applicable under the latest SSC notification.

Eligibility

Requirement General Information
Educational Qualification Bachelor's degree from a recognised university or an equivalent qualification, subject to the applicable SSC CPO notification.
Age The prescribed age limit and cut-off date are specified in the latest SSC CPO notification. Age relaxation is available to eligible categories as per government rules.
Nationality Nationality/citizenship conditions are specified in the applicable SSC CPO recruitment rules.
Driving Licence A valid driving licence for LMV may be required for the Delhi Police Sub-Inspector post, subject to the latest SSC CPO notification and applicable conditions.
Physical Standards Candidates must satisfy the prescribed physical standards and physical endurance requirements applicable to the post and category.

Eligibility conditions, age limits, driving licence requirements and physical standards can change. Always verify the latest SSC CPO notification before applying.

Physical Standard Test — PST

Requirement General Information
Height Prescribed minimum height standards apply according to gender, category and the post. Certain relaxations may be available under the applicable rules.
Chest Chest measurement requirements, including minimum expansion where applicable, are prescribed for eligible male candidates according to the relevant rules.
Weight Weight should generally be proportionate to height and age according to the applicable medical and recruitment standards.
Eyesight Vision and other medical requirements are checked according to the standards prescribed for the concerned post and force.
Physical Fitness Candidates must meet the prescribed physical standards before proceeding to the applicable stages of recruitment.

Physical standards differ according to post, gender and category. Candidates should always check the latest SSC CPO notification for exact measurements and permitted relaxations.

Physical Endurance Test — PET

Activity Purpose
Running Tests cardiovascular endurance and running ability according to the prescribed standard.
Long Jump Tests lower-body strength, coordination and physical agility.
High Jump Tests strength, flexibility, coordination and jumping ability where applicable.
Other Physical Activities Additional activities or standards may apply according to the post, force and latest recruitment rules.

PET is qualifying in nature. The exact events, distances, timings and standards should be checked from the latest SSC CPO notification.

SSC CPO क्या है?

Staff Selection Commission Central Police Organization (SSC CPO) परीक्षा Staff Selection Commission द्वारा Delhi Police और Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) में Sub-Inspector पदों पर भर्ती के लिए आयोजित की जाती है।

इस परीक्षा के माध्यम से योग्य candidates को Sub-Inspector के रूप में police और central armed police organisations में सेवा करने का अवसर मिलता है। Recruitment process में सामान्यतः Computer Based Examination, Physical Standard Test/Physical Endurance Test, Paper-II, Medical Examination और Document Verification जैसे stages शामिल होते हैं। Exact process latest SSC notification के अनुसार निर्धारित होता है।
