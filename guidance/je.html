What is SSC JE?

The Staff Selection Commission Junior Engineer (SSC JE) Examination is conducted by SSC for recruitment to Junior Engineer posts in various Government of India departments and organizations.

The examination covers Civil, Mechanical and Electrical Engineering disciplines. Candidates are selected through Paper-I and Paper-II, followed by the applicable document verification and other prescribed requirements.

SSC's official website lists the examination as the Junior Engineer (Civil, Mechanical & Electrical) Examination. The Commission's 2026 calendar lists the Junior Engineer Examination 2026 with a scheduled date of 31 March 2026. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

SSC JE Exam Pattern

Stage Paper Marks Duration
Paper-I General Intelligence & Reasoning + General Awareness + General Engineering 200 2 Hours
Paper-I General Engineering is based on the candidate's selected discipline — Civil, Mechanical or Electrical. Part of 200 Marks Included in 2 Hours
Paper-II General Engineering — Civil / Mechanical / Electrical 300 2 Hours
Paper-II Computer Based Objective Type Examination 300 2 Hours
Total Paper-I + Paper-II 500 4 Hours

SSC JE examination consists of two papers. Paper-I is a Computer Based Examination and Paper-II is also conducted in Computer Based Mode. The exact scheme, negative marking and qualifying requirements should be checked in the latest SSC JE notification.

Preparation Strategy

📚

Understand the Syllabus

Start by studying the complete SSC JE syllabus and divide technical subjects into small, manageable topics.

🎯

Focus on Technical Subjects

Give major preparation time to your selected engineering branch because the technical section carries the largest share of marks.

📝

Solve Previous Year Questions

Practise SSC JE previous year questions topic-wise to understand repeated concepts and question patterns.

🧮

Practise Numericals

Regularly solve numerical problems from technical subjects and maintain a separate formula and shortcut notebook.

🧠

Improve Reasoning

Practise analogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, non-verbal reasoning and other SSC-level reasoning topics.

📰

Revise General Awareness

Study static GK, General Science, history, geography, polity, economics and important current affairs.

⏱️

Take Mock Tests

Attempt full-length mock tests within the actual examination time and analyse every incorrect and skipped question.

🔄

Regular Revision

Revise formulas, short notes, important facts and previously solved questions at regular intervals.

SSC JE क्या है?

SSC Junior Engineer (JE) परीक्षा Staff Selection Commission (SSC) द्वारा विभिन्न Government Departments और Organisations में Junior Engineer पदों पर भर्ती के लिए आयोजित की जाती है।

यह परीक्षा मुख्य रूप से Civil, Mechanical और Electrical Engineering disciplines से संबंधित उम्मीदवारों के लिए आयोजित की जाती है। SSC JE में उम्मीदवारों की General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness और संबंधित Engineering discipline की technical knowledge का परीक्षण किया जाता है।

02

Mechanical Engineering

Machines, thermodynamics, manufacturing, production, maintenance और mechanical systems से संबंधित engineering branch।

03

Electrical Engineering

Electrical circuits, machines, power systems, electrical installations, measurements और control systems से संबंधित branch।

Important Tips for SSC JE

📖

Official Syllabus Follow करें

Latest SSC syllabus को सामने रखकर हर topic को systematically पूरा करें और random topics पढ़ने से बचें।

🔢

Technical Numericals करें

Technical subjects में formulas, concepts, units और numerical calculations की नियमित practice करें।

📄

Previous Year Papers लगाएं

PYQs से difficulty level, repeated topics और actual examination pattern को समझने में मदद मिलती है।

⏰

Time Management

Timer लगाकर practice करें और easy, moderate तथा difficult questions को attempt करने की सही strategy बनाएं।

📒

Short Notes बनाएं

Important formulas, difficult concepts और बार-बार होने वाली mistakes के लिए short notes तैयार करें।

🔁

Regular Revision करें

Daily और weekly revision को preparation का हिस्सा बनाएं ताकि पुराने topics लंबे समय तक याद रहें।
