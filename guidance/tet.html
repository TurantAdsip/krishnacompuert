What is TET?

Teacher Eligibility Test (TET) is a teacher eligibility examination conducted by the appropriate government or examination authority. TET is used as part of the teacher eligibility/recruitment framework for the applicable school levels and recruitment rules.

At the national level, CTET is conducted by CBSE, while States/UTs may conduct their own TET examinations according to the applicable rules. NCTE provides the framework and minimum qualification notifications for teachers. citeturn0search0turn0search2

State TET and CTET

Exam Conducting Authority Main Scope
CTET CBSE Central-level teacher eligibility framework for applicable schools
State TET Concerned State Government / Examination Authority Teacher eligibility for the concerned State according to its rules

Do not assume that every State TET has exactly the same eligibility, syllabus, language, duration or qualifying rules. Always read the latest notification of the concerned State.

Eligibility

Level General Guidance
Primary Stage Eligibility generally depends on prescribed school-level teacher education qualifications such as relevant D.El.Ed./B.El.Ed. pathways and the applicable State rules.
Upper Primary Stage Eligibility generally depends on the prescribed graduation and teacher-education qualification pathways and applicable State rules.
State-specific Rules Exact qualification, percentage, subject combination, age requirements and other conditions must be checked in the latest State notification.
Final Verification Passing/appearing in a TET does not by itself guarantee appointment. Final eligibility is verified according to the applicable recruitment rules. citeturn0search1

TET Certificate and Recruitment

A TET qualification is an eligibility component for applicable teacher recruitment; it is not itself a guarantee of appointment. Recruitment depends on vacancies, recruitment rules, merit/selection procedure and document verification of the concerned authority.

The exact validity period and certificate provisions should be checked in the latest applicable State notification. Do not assume that every State uses identical certificate rules.

TET क्या है?

Teacher Eligibility Test (TET) एक शिक्षक पात्रता परीक्षा है, जिसका आयोजन संबंधित राज्य सरकार या परीक्षा प्राधिकरण द्वारा किया जा सकता है। TET का उपयोग लागू teacher eligibility और recruitment framework के अंतर्गत किया जाता है।

CTET को CBSE आयोजित करता है, जबकि विभिन्न States अपनी State TET परीक्षाएँ आयोजित कर सकते हैं। NCTE teachers की minimum qualifications और TET से संबंधित framework उपलब्ध कराता है। citeturn0search0turn0search2
