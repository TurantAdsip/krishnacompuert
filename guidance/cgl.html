Complete SSC CGL guidance — eligibility, exam pattern, posts, subjects, books, selection process and preparation strategy.

What is SSC CGL?

The Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level (SSC CGL) Examination is conducted by the Staff Selection Commission for recruitment to various Group B and Group C posts in different Ministries, Departments, organisations and offices of the Government of India.

The examination is conducted for graduates who meet the eligibility conditions prescribed in the latest SSC CGL notification.

Major Posts Through SSC CGL

01

Assistant Section Officer

Posts in various Central Government ministries and departments, subject to the posts notified in the relevant examination year.

02

Inspector of Income Tax

Group B post associated with the Income Tax Department, subject to vacancies and applicable recruitment rules.

03

Inspector (Central Excise)

Inspector-level post under the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs, as notified.

04

Assistant Enforcement Officer

Post under the Enforcement Directorate, subject to the vacancies and conditions specified in the notification.

05

Sub Inspector

Sub Inspector posts in notified departments such as CBI, subject to applicable eligibility and vacancies.

06

Divisional Accountant

Account-related Group B post in participating government offices, according to the notified vacancies.

The exact posts, departments, pay levels and vacancies can change from one SSC CGL examination year to another. Always check the latest SSC notification.

Eligibility

Requirement General Information
Educational Qualification A bachelor's degree from a recognised university or equivalent qualification is generally required, subject to post-specific conditions.
Final Year Candidates Final year candidates may be eligible subject to the qualification cut-off date and conditions specified in the latest SSC CGL notification.
Age Limit Age limits vary according to the post. The exact age limit and cut-off date are given in the latest notification.
Nationality Nationality/citizenship conditions are specified in the applicable SSC rules and notification.
Special Qualification Some posts may have specific educational or subject requirements. Check the latest notification for post-wise eligibility.

Eligibility conditions can vary by post and examination year. Always verify the latest SSC CGL notification before applying.

Tier-I Subjects

Subject Important Areas
General Intelligence & Reasoning Analogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, syllogism, Venn diagrams, spatial reasoning and logical reasoning.
General Awareness History, Geography, Polity, Economy, Science, Current Affairs and general knowledge.
Quantitative Aptitude Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, data interpretation and numerical ability.
English Comprehension Vocabulary, grammar, sentence correction, comprehension, cloze test, synonyms, antonyms and usage.

Tier-II Paper-I Subjects

Area What to Study
Mathematical Abilities Number system, arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, statistics and related quantitative topics.
Reasoning & General Intelligence Verbal and non-verbal reasoning, analogy, classification, series, logical reasoning and problem solving.
English Language & Comprehension Grammar, vocabulary, comprehension, sentence structure, error detection and practical English usage.
General Awareness Current events, history, geography, polity, economy, science and general knowledge.
Computer Knowledge Basic computer concepts, hardware, software, internet, networking, cybersecurity and common applications.
Data Entry Speed Test Qualifying data-entry related skill test for candidates/posts to which it applies under the current scheme.

Tier-II Paper-II — Statistics

Area What to Study
Collection & Classification of Data Collection, classification and presentation of statistical data.
Measures of Central Tendency Mean, median, mode and related concepts.
Measures of Dispersion Range, quartile deviation, mean deviation and standard deviation.
Correlation & Regression Correlation, regression analysis and related statistical concepts.
Probability Basic probability concepts and probability distributions.
Sampling Theory Sampling methods and basic concepts of statistical sampling.

Paper-II is applicable to candidates for posts for which the Statistics paper is prescribed under the latest SSC CGL scheme.

Tier-II Paper-III — General Studies (Finance & Economics)

Area What to Study
Finance Financial institutions, financial markets, accounting and related financial concepts.
Economics Basic economic concepts, demand and supply, production, national income and economic development.
Indian Economy Structure of the Indian economy, public finance, monetary system and major economic policies.
Government Schemes Important government programmes and economic initiatives relevant to the prescribed syllabus.
Accounting Basic principles of accounting, financial statements and fundamental accounting concepts.

Paper-III is applicable to candidates for specified posts according to the latest SSC CGL examination scheme.

Selection Process

01

Tier-I

Computer-based objective examination covering Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude and English.

02

Tier-II

Computer-based examination with Paper-I and applicable additional papers/modules according to the latest scheme.

03

Skill / Qualifying Tests

Computer Knowledge Test, Data Entry Speed Test or other applicable qualifying requirements may apply according to the post and latest scheme.

04

Document Verification

Educational, identity, category and other required documents are verified according to SSC instructions.

05

Post Preference

Post and department preferences are considered according to the procedure and instructions applicable to the examination year.

06

Final Allocation

Final allocation is made according to merit, preferences, vacancies, eligibility and applicable recruitment rules.

🧠

⏱️

SSC CGL क्या है?

Staff Selection Commission Combined Graduate Level (SSC CGL) Examination, Staff Selection Commission द्वारा भारत सरकार के विभिन्न Ministries, Departments, organisations और offices में विभिन्न Group B और Group C posts पर भर्ती के लिए आयोजित की जाती है।

यह परीक्षा उन graduates के लिए आयोजित की जाती है जो latest SSC CGL notification में निर्धारित eligibility conditions को पूरा करते हैं।

Selection Process

01

Tier-I

Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude और English के objective questions की Computer Based Examination।

02

Tier-II

Latest scheme के अनुसार Paper-I तथा applicable additional papers/modules की Computer Based Examination।

03

Skill / Qualifying Tests

Post और latest scheme के अनुसार Computer Knowledge Test, Data Entry Speed Test या अन्य applicable qualifying requirements हो सकते हैं।

04

Document Verification

SSC instructions के अनुसार educational, identity, category और अन्य आवश्यक documents verify किए जाते हैं।

05

Post Preference

Examination year में लागू procedure और instructions के अनुसार post तथा department preferences पर विचार किया जाता है।

06

Final Allocation

Merit, preferences, vacancies, eligibility और applicable recruitment rules के अनुसार final allocation किया जाता है।
