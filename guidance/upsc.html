What is UPSC?

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) conducts the Civil Services Examination for recruitment to various services of the Government of India.

UPSC Examination Stages

01

Preliminary Examination

Objective type examination.

02

Main Examination

Written descriptive examination.

03

Personality Test

Interview conducted by UPSC.

UPSC Optional Subjects

Candidates have to choose one optional subject for the Main Examination. The optional subject has two papers.

Agriculture Anthropology Geography History Political Science Public Administration Sociology Economics Philosophy Psychology Commerce & Accountancy Law Mathematics Physics Chemistry Medical Science

UPSC Eligibility

Educational Qualification

Candidates must have a bachelor's degree from a recognised university or an equivalent qualification.

Age Limit

The applicable age limit and relaxations are specified in the latest UPSC notification.

Documents Required

  • Photo ID Proof
  • Educational Certificates
  • Photograph
  • Signature
  • Category Certificate, if applicable

पेपर III सामान्य अध्ययन-II – शासन, संविधान, राजव्यवस्था, सामाजिक न्याय एवं अंतरराष्ट्रीय संबंध

UPSC वैकल्पिक विषय

उम्मीदवारों को मुख्य परीक्षा के लिए एक वैकल्पिक विषय चुनना होता है। वैकल्पिक विषय के दो पेपर होते हैं।

कृषि मानव विज्ञान भूगोल इतिहास राजनीति विज्ञान लोक प्रशासन समाजशास्त्र अर्थशास्त्र दर्शनशास्त्र मनोविज्ञान वाणिज्य एवं लेखाशास्त्र विधि गणित भौतिक विज्ञान रसायन विज्ञान चिकित्सा विज्ञान
