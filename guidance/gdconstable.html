What is SSC GD Constable?

The Staff Selection Commission General Duty (GD) Constable Examination is conducted by SSC for recruitment to Constable (GD) posts in various Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), Secretariat Security Force (SSF), and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles.

The examination is a national-level recruitment examination. Vacancies, force-wise posts, eligibility conditions and other details are specified in the latest SSC GD Constable notification. The 2026 examination was scheduled by SSC from April to May 2026, with the examination schedule subsequently revised through official notices. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

Forces / Posts

01

BSF

Border Security Force — responsible for guarding India's international borders and performing assigned security duties.

02

CISF

Central Industrial Security Force — provides security to important industrial installations and other designated establishments.

03

CRPF

Central Reserve Police Force — performs internal security, law and order and other duties assigned by the Government.

04

ITBP

Indo-Tibetan Border Police — performs border guarding and other security duties in difficult terrain.

05

SSB

Sashastra Seema Bal — performs border guarding and other duties assigned under its mandate.

06

Assam Rifles

Recruitment to Rifleman (GD) posts in Assam Rifles is included in the SSC GD recruitment process.

07

SSF

Secretariat Security Force — provides security duties at designated government establishments.

The exact force-wise vacancies, reservation, vacancies for male/female candidates and other post-related details depend on the latest SSC GD Constable notification.

Eligibility

Requirement General Information
Educational Qualification Candidate should have passed Matriculation (10th Standard) or an equivalent examination from a recognised Board as prescribed in the latest SSC GD notification.
Age Limit The applicable age limit and cut-off date are specified in the latest SSC GD Constable notification. Age relaxation is available to eligible categories according to Government rules.
Nationality Nationality/citizenship conditions are specified in the applicable SSC rules and the recruitment notification.
Physical Standards Eligible candidates must satisfy the prescribed physical standards applicable to the recruitment, including height and other requirements as specified by SSC.
Medical Standards Selected candidates must satisfy the prescribed medical standards applicable to the concerned force and post.

Eligibility, age, physical standards and other conditions can change between recruitment cycles. Always verify the latest official SSC GD Constable notification before applying.

SSC GD Constable Syllabus

Subject What to Study
General Intelligence and Reasoning Analogy, similarities and differences, spatial visualization, spatial orientation, visual memory, observation, relationship concepts, arithmetical reasoning, figural classification, number series, non-verbal series, coding-decoding and other basic reasoning topics.
General Knowledge and General Awareness Current events, India and neighbouring countries, sports, history, culture, geography, economic scene, general polity, Indian Constitution, scientific research and general knowledge.
Elementary Mathematics Number system, whole numbers, decimals, fractions, fundamental arithmetic operations, percentage, ratio and proportion, average, interest, profit and loss, discount, mensuration, time and distance, ratio and time, time and work and basic arithmetic concepts.
English / Hindi Basic understanding of English or Hindi language, vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, comprehension and correct usage.

Physical Standard Test (PST)

Standard General Information
Height Prescribed minimum height standards apply according to gender, category, region and other conditions specified in the latest notification.
Chest Chest standards, where applicable, are prescribed for male candidates with applicable relaxations according to the recruitment rules.
Weight Weight should generally be proportionate to height and age according to the applicable medical and recruitment standards.

Exact physical standards and category-wise relaxations must be verified from the latest SSC GD Constable notification and applicable CAPF medical standards.

Selection Process

01

Computer Based Examination

The first stage is the Computer Based Examination covering General Intelligence and Reasoning, General Knowledge and General Awareness, Elementary Mathematics and English/Hindi.

02

Physical Efficiency Test

Candidates qualifying the written examination are called for the Physical Efficiency Test according to the applicable SSC GD recruitment rules.

03

Physical Standard Test

Candidates must satisfy the prescribed physical standards such as height, chest and other applicable requirements.

04

Medical Examination

Candidates are required to meet the prescribed medical standards applicable to the concerned force and post.

05

Document Verification

Educational, identity, category and other required documents are verified according to SSC instructions.

06

Final Selection

Final selection and force allocation are made according to merit, vacancies, preferences where applicable, eligibility and the rules applicable to the recruitment year.

Staff Selection Commission General Duty (GD) Constable Examination, SSC द्वारा विभिन्न Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), Secretariat Security Force (SSF) में Constable (GD) तथा Assam Rifles में Rifleman (GD) के पदों पर भर्ती के लिए आयोजित की जाती है।

06

Final Selection
