Combined Medical Services Examination (CMS)

Complete CMS guidance — eligibility, exam pattern, subjects, books, selection process, medical examination and preparation strategy.

What is CMS?

The Combined Medical Services Examination (CMS) is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) for recruitment to various Central Government medical posts.

The examination is intended for medical graduates who meet the eligibility conditions prescribed in the latest UPSC CMS notification and the applicable rules.

Posts Generally Covered

01

Medical Officer

Medical officer posts under Central Government organisations/services as specified in the applicable notification.

02

Assistant Divisional Medical Officer

Medical officer posts associated with the railway medical services, subject to the vacancies notified.

03

General Duty Medical Officer

General duty medical posts in participating Central Government departments and organisations.

04

Other Notified Medical Posts

Other eligible posts may be included according to the latest UPSC CMS notification and vacancies.

The exact posts, departments, services and number of vacancies can change from one examination year to another. Always check the latest UPSC notification.

Eligibility

Requirement General Information
Educational Qualification Pass in the written and practical parts of the final MBBS examination, subject to the conditions specified in the latest notification.
Final Year Candidates Candidates appearing in the final MBBS examination may be eligible subject to the conditions and deadlines specified by UPSC.
Age The exact age limit and cut-off date are specified in the latest UPSC CMS notification.
Nationality Nationality/citizenship conditions are specified in the applicable CMS rules.
Medical Standards Candidates must satisfy the prescribed medical standards applicable to the relevant service/post.

Eligibility conditions can change. Always verify the latest UPSC Combined Medical Services Examination notification and applicable rules before applying.

CMS Exam Pattern

Stage Paper Marks Duration
Written Examination Paper I — General Medicine 250 2 Hours
Written Examination Paper II — Surgery, Gynaecology & Obstetrics, Preventive & Social Medicine 250 2 Hours
Written Examination Total Paper I + Paper II 500 4 Hours
Personality Test Interview / Personality Test 100 —
Final Total Written Examination + Personality Test 600 —

The written examination consists of two objective papers. The exact scheme, marking provisions and other conditions should be checked in the latest UPSC CMS notification.

Paper I — General Medicine

Area What to Study
General Medicine Common medical disorders, diagnosis, management and important clinical conditions.
Cardiology Heart diseases, hypertension, ischemic heart disease and common cardiac emergencies.
Respiratory Medicine Respiratory infections, asthma, COPD, tuberculosis and respiratory emergencies.
Gastroenterology Gastrointestinal, liver, biliary and pancreatic disorders.
Neurology Neurological disorders, stroke, seizures and common neurological emergencies.
Nephrology Renal diseases, electrolyte disorders and kidney-related emergencies.
Endocrinology Diabetes, thyroid disorders and common endocrine diseases.
Infectious Diseases Common infections, communicable diseases and important tropical diseases.
Pediatrics Common childhood diseases, growth and development, nutrition and pediatric emergencies.

Paper II — Surgery

Area What to Study
General Surgery Principles of surgery, common surgical conditions and operative management.
Trauma Shock, wounds, fractures, burns and emergency trauma management.
Orthopaedics Common bone, joint and musculoskeletal conditions and injuries.
Urology Common urinary tract disorders and urological emergencies.
Ophthalmology Common eye diseases, injuries and basic emergency management.
ENT Common ear, nose and throat disorders and their management.

Paper II — Gynaecology & Obstetrics

Area What to Study
Obstetrics Normal pregnancy, antenatal care, labour, delivery and postpartum care.
High-Risk Pregnancy Pregnancy complications, obstetric emergencies and their management.
Gynaecology Common gynaecological disorders, diagnosis and management.
Family Planning Contraceptive methods, family planning services and reproductive health.
Infertility Basic causes, evaluation and management of male and female infertility.
Gynaecological Oncology Common cancers of the female reproductive system and basic management.

Paper II — Preventive & Social Medicine

Area What to Study
Epidemiology Basic concepts, measures of disease frequency, study designs and screening.
Biostatistics Basic statistical methods, averages, dispersion and interpretation of health data.
Communicable Diseases Important communicable diseases, prevention, control and national health programmes.
Non-Communicable Diseases Major chronic diseases, risk factors, prevention and control strategies.
Maternal & Child Health Maternal health, child health, immunisation, nutrition and related programmes.
Nutrition Macronutrients, micronutrients, deficiency diseases and public-health nutrition.
Environmental Health Water, sanitation, housing, air pollution and occupational/environmental health.
Health Administration Health services, national health programmes, primary health care and health planning.

NCERT Books for CMS

Area NCERT Use Priority

General Science Can help with basic science concepts where relevant. Low

Important: NCERT is not the main source for CMS preparation. CMS requires MBBS-level medical knowledge and preparation according to the UPSC syllabus.

Selection Process

01

Written Examination

Two objective papers covering General Medicine, Surgery, Gynaecology & Obstetrics and Preventive & Social Medicine.

02

Written Qualification

Candidates who meet the applicable written-stage requirements are considered for the next stage.

03

Personality Test

Shortlisted candidates appear for the UPSC Personality Test/Interview.

04

Document Verification

Required educational, identity and other documents are verified according to UPSC instructions.

05

Medical Examination

Candidates must satisfy the prescribed medical standards for the relevant post/service.

06

Final Selection

Final selection is made according to applicable rules, merit, vacancies and eligibility.

CMS क्या है?

Combined Medical Services Examination (CMS) UPSC द्वारा Central Government की विभिन्न medical posts पर भर्ती के लिए आयोजित की जाने वाली परीक्षा है।

यह परीक्षा उन medical graduates के लिए है जो latest UPSC CMS notification और applicable rules में निर्धारित eligibility conditions को पूरा करते हैं।

सामान्यतः शामिल Posts

01

Medical Officer

Latest notification में निर्धारित Central Government organisations/services के अंतर्गत Medical Officer posts।

02

Assistant Divisional Medical Officer

Notified vacancies के अनुसार railway medical services से संबंधित medical officer posts।

03

General Duty Medical Officer

Participating Central Government departments और organisations में General Duty Medical Officer posts।

04

Other Notified Medical Posts

Latest UPSC CMS notification और vacancies के अनुसार अन्य eligible medical posts भी शामिल हो सकती हैं।

Posts, departments, services और vacancies हर examination year में बदल सकते हैं। हमेशा latest UPSC notification देखें।
