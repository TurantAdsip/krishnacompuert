CAPF Assistant Commandants Exam Guidance

Complete guidance for UPSC Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination — eligibility, subjects, books, NCERT, exam pattern, physical standards, PET, medical and preparation.

What is CAPF (ACs)?

The Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination is conducted by the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) for recruitment to the post of Assistant Commandant in the Central Armed Police Forces, according to the vacancies and conditions notified for the examination.

The forces covered under the examination include BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP and SSB, subject to the applicable notification.

CAPF Eligibility

Requirement General Information
Educational Qualification A Bachelor's degree from a recognised university or institution is required, subject to the conditions in the current notification.
Age The age limit and exact date-of-birth window are specified in each UPSC notification. Category-wise relaxations may apply.
Nationality Nationality/citizenship conditions are specified by UPSC in the examination notification.
Gender Both male and female candidates may apply, subject to the conditions of the notification.
Medical Standards Candidates must meet the prescribed physical and medical standards.

Important: Do not publish a fixed age range without checking the latest UPSC notification. The exact DOB window can change from one examination year to another.

CAPF Written Exam Pattern

Paper Subject Type Marks Duration
Paper I General Ability and Intelligence Objective 250 2 Hours
Paper II General Studies, Essay and Comprehension Descriptive 200 3 Hours
Total Both Papers 450 5 Hours

Paper II includes essay, précis writing, comprehension and other language/analytical components as prescribed by UPSC. Candidates should follow the latest syllabus and instructions.

CAPF Subjects

Paper Subject / Section Main Areas
Paper I General Ability and Intelligence Logical reasoning, quantitative aptitude, data interpretation, general science, current affairs, history, geography and polity/economy
Paper II Essay National issues, security, social/economic issues, governance and current affairs
Paper II General Studies Indian polity, economy, history, geography, security and current events
Paper II Comprehension Reading comprehension, précis writing, communication and analytical writing

Selection Process

01

Written Exam

Paper I and Paper II conducted by UPSC.

02

PET / PST

Candidates qualifying the written stage undergo the prescribed physical standards and physical efficiency tests.

03

Medical

Medical examination according to the applicable service standards.

04

Interview / Personality Test

Personality assessment and interview for candidates who qualify the earlier stages.

05

Final Merit

Final selection is based on applicable written, interview and other prescribed requirements.

Physical Standards & PET

CAPF requires candidates to meet prescribed physical standards and qualify the Physical Efficiency Test. Standards can differ by gender and may have specific relaxations or service-wise conditions.

Area Preparation
Running Build endurance gradually with regular running and timed practice.
Long Jump Practise technique, leg strength and landing safely.
Shot Put Where applicable, practise technique and strength under proper guidance.
General Fitness Strength, mobility, stamina and healthy body composition.

Important: Do not use unofficial height, chest, running-time or other physical figures as final rules. Always check the latest UPSC notification and medical/physical standards.

Documents Required

  • 10th certificate / matriculation document for date-of-birth proof as prescribed
  • Degree certificate / provisional degree and marksheets
  • Valid photo identity details
  • Category certificate, if applicable
  • Other certificates required for age, education, reservation or relaxation, where applicable
  • Original documents for verification at later stages as instructed by UPSC

CAPF क्या है?

इस examination में BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP और SSB शामिल होते हैं, subject to the applicable notification.
