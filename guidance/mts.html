Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff (MTS)

Complete SSC MTS guidance — eligibility, exam pattern, subjects, books, selection process and preparation strategy.

What is SSC MTS?

The Staff Selection Commission Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff Examination is conducted by the Staff Selection Commission for recruitment to Multi-Tasking Staff posts in various Ministries, Departments and Offices of the Government of India.

SSC MTS is a national-level recruitment examination generally conducted for eligible candidates who have passed the required level of education specified in the latest notification.

SSC MTS Post

01

Multi-Tasking Staff

Non-technical Group C posts in various Central Government Ministries, Departments, Offices and other participating organisations.

02

General Office Duties

MTS personnel may perform assigned office support and routine duties according to the requirements of the concerned department.

03

Departmental Responsibilities

Job responsibilities depend on the department, office and nature of duties assigned to the selected candidate.

Exact vacancies, departments, age conditions, pay level and other post-related details are based on the latest SSC MTS notification.

Eligibility

Requirement General Information
Educational Qualification Candidate should have passed Matriculation (10th Standard) or an equivalent examination from a recognised Board as prescribed in the latest notification.
Age Limit The applicable age limit and cut-off date are specified in the latest SSC MTS notification and may vary for particular categories or posts.
Nationality Nationality/citizenship conditions are specified under the applicable SSC rules and notification.
Special Conditions Additional conditions, including post-wise or examination-year-specific requirements, should be checked in the latest official notification.

Eligibility conditions can change according to the recruitment year. Always verify the latest official SSC MTS notification before applying.

SSC MTS Exam Pattern

Session Subject Questions Marks Duration
Session-I Numerical and Mathematical Ability 20 60 45 Minutes
Session-I Reasoning Ability and Problem Solving 20 60
Session-II General Awareness 25 75 45 Minutes
Session-II English Language and Comprehension 25 75

The examination scheme, marking and session-wise details should always be verified from the latest official SSC MTS notification.

SSC MTS Subjects

Subject Important Areas
Numerical and Mathematical Ability Number system, integers, whole numbers, fundamental arithmetic operations, percentage, ratio, average, profit and loss, simple interest, time and work, time and distance, basic geometry and data interpretation.
Reasoning Ability and Problem Solving Analogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, similarities and differences, directions, age problems, calendar, clock, non-verbal reasoning and problem solving.
General Awareness History, Geography, Culture, Polity, Economics, General Science, Environment and Current Affairs.
English Language and Comprehension Basic English grammar, vocabulary, sentence structure, synonyms, antonyms, comprehension and correct usage.

Recommended Books — SSC MTS

Subject Suggested Material Purpose
English Standard SSC English grammar and vocabulary book + previous year questions Grammar, vocabulary and comprehension practice
Reasoning Standard SSC Reasoning book + previous year questions Logical reasoning and speed practice
Mathematics Standard SSC Mathematics book + previous year questions Basic concepts, formulas and calculation practice
General Awareness General Knowledge reference book + NCERTs + current affairs Static GK, basic concepts and current affairs
Previous Year Papers Official SSC question papers and reliable PYQ practice material Understand question trends and improve accuracy

Book names are not mandatory. Use reliable standard books, the latest syllabus and previous year questions. Avoid using too many books for the same subject.

Selection Process

01

Computer Based Examination

The SSC MTS Computer Based Examination is conducted in two sessions covering Numerical and Mathematical Ability, Reasoning Ability and Problem Solving, General Awareness and English Language and Comprehension.

02

Session-I

Session-I covers Numerical and Mathematical Ability and Reasoning Ability and Problem Solving.

03

Session-II

Session-II covers General Awareness and English Language and Comprehension.

04

Document Verification

Educational, identity, category and other required documents are verified according to SSC instructions.

05

Post / Department Allocation

Allocation is carried out according to merit, vacancies, preferences where applicable, eligibility and the rules applicable to the examination year.

06

Final Appointment

Final appointment is subject to document verification, eligibility, applicable recruitment rules and other prescribed conditions.

Preparation Strategy

📚

Build Strong Basics

Strengthen Mathematics, Reasoning, English and General Awareness fundamentals before moving to intensive practice.

📝

Solve PYQs

Practise previous year SSC MTS questions topic-wise and identify frequently tested concepts.

➗

Daily Mathematics

Practise calculations, arithmetic and basic mathematical concepts regularly to improve speed and accuracy.

📖

Improve English

Revise grammar, vocabulary, comprehension and sentence-based questions regularly.

🧠

Reasoning Practice

Practise different reasoning patterns and develop the ability to solve questions accurately within limited time.

⏱️

Mock Tests

Take regular mock tests, analyse mistakes and improve time management and question selection.

Documents Generally Required

  • Matriculation/10th certificate or other prescribed document for proof of date of birth
  • 10th Standard educational qualification certificate and marksheet
  • Valid photo identity document
  • Category certificate, if applicable
  • Disability certificate, if applicable
  • Required documents for special eligibility conditions, wherever applicable
  • Other certificates/documents required according to the latest SSC instructions
