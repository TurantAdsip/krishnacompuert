What is IBPS SO?

IBPS Specialist Officer (SO) is a national-level banking recruitment examination conducted by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) for recruitment to Specialist Officer posts in participating public sector banks.

The recruitment is conducted through the Common Recruitment Process (CRP-SPL) for specialist posts such as IT Officer, Agricultural Field Officer, Rajbhasha Adhikari, Law Officer, HR/Personnel Officer and Marketing Officer, as prescribed in the applicable IBPS notification.

IBPS SO provides opportunities for candidates with specialised educational qualifications to work in specialist roles within participating public sector banks. Candidates are selected through the stages prescribed in the latest IBPS CRP-SPL notification.

Important: The participating banks, vacancies, eligibility criteria, specialist posts, examination pattern, selection process and other conditions may change according to the latest IBPS CRP-SPL notification. Candidates should always check the latest official IBPS notification before applying.

IBPS SO Specialist Posts

💻

IT Officer

Responsible for specialist IT-related activities such as computer systems, networking, databases, information security, software and technology-related banking operations.

🌾

Works on agricultural and rural banking activities, including agricultural finance, credit requirements, rural development and related banking services.

⚖️

Law Officer

Handles legal matters related to banking operations, documentation, regulations, legal compliance and other law-related activities of the bank.

📝

Works on official language-related activities, translation, implementation of language policies and Hindi language work within the banking organisation.

👥

HR / Personnel Officer

Handles human-resource-related activities such as employee administration, recruitment support, training, personnel policies and other HR functions.

📢

Marketing Officer

Works on banking product promotion, marketing strategies, customer acquisition, market analysis and other marketing-related activities.

Important Note: IBPS SO recruitment is conducted for specialist officer posts notified under the applicable CRP-SPL recruitment cycle. The available posts, participating banks, vacancies and eligibility requirements may vary according to the latest official IBPS notification.

Eligibility

Eligibility Criteria Details
Nationality / Citizenship The candidate must satisfy the nationality/citizenship conditions prescribed in the latest official IBPS CRP-SPL notification.
Age Limit The candidate must generally be between 20 and 30 years of age, subject to the cut-off date and conditions prescribed in the latest IBPS SO notification. Age relaxation is applicable to eligible reserved categories as per Government and IBPS rules.
Educational Qualification The candidate must possess the required educational qualification for the particular Specialist Officer post applied for. The qualification varies according to the specialist discipline such as IT Officer, Agricultural Field Officer, Rajbhasha Adhikari, Law Officer, HR/Personnel Officer and Marketing Officer.
IT Officer Relevant qualification in Computer Science, Computer Applications, Information Technology, Electronics, Electronics & Communication or another prescribed related discipline, as specified in the latest notification.
Agricultural Field Officer Four-year degree in Agriculture or a prescribed related agricultural discipline such as Horticulture, Veterinary Science, Dairy Science, Animal Husbandry, Agricultural Engineering, Food Technology, Biotechnology or other eligible disciplines mentioned in the notification.
Rajbhasha Adhikari Post Graduate degree in Hindi with English as a subject at the degree level, or Post Graduate degree in Sanskrit with Hindi and English as subjects at the degree level, subject to the conditions prescribed by IBPS.
Law Officer A Bachelor Degree in Law (LL.B.) and enrolment as an advocate with the Bar Council, or other qualification/conditions prescribed in the latest IBPS SO notification.
HR / Personnel Officer Graduate degree along with a prescribed full-time Post Graduate degree or diploma in Human Resource Management, Personnel Management, Industrial Relations, Labour Law or another eligible related discipline as specified by IBPS.
Marketing Officer Graduate degree along with a prescribed full-time MBA/PGDBM/PGDM or equivalent qualification with specialisation in Marketing, as specified in the applicable IBPS SO notification.
Educational Qualification Date The required qualification must have been obtained by the date specified in the applicable IBPS SO notification. Candidates should carefully verify the exact qualification and passing-date requirements before applying.
Age Relaxation Eligible candidates belonging to categories such as SC/ST, OBC (Non-Creamy Layer), Persons with Benchmark Disabilities, Ex-Servicemen and other specified categories may receive relaxation in the upper age limit according to applicable Government and IBPS rules.
Category Requirements Candidates claiming reservation, age relaxation or any other applicable benefit must possess valid certificates and supporting documents in the prescribed format.
Other Requirements The candidate must satisfy all other conditions prescribed in the latest IBPS CRP-SPL notification, including educational qualification, age, nationality, document verification and other recruitment requirements.

Important Note: IBPS SO eligibility is post-specific. Educational qualifications are different for different Specialist Officer posts, so candidates must carefully check the exact qualification, age cut-off date, passing-date requirement and other conditions in the latest official IBPS CRP-SPL notification before applying.

Important Note: The IBPS SO Preliminary Examination pattern differs according to the Specialist Officer post. For Law Officer and Rajbhasha Adhikari, General Awareness with Special Reference to Banking Industry is conducted instead of Quantitative Aptitude.

Main Examination: For IT Officer, Agriculture Field Officer, HR/Personnel Officer and Marketing Officer, the Main Examination consists of English Language, Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude, Professional Knowledge — Objective and a Descriptive Paper in English. Interview: Candidates shortlisted after the Online Main Examination are called for the Interview. The Interview carries 100 marks. The minimum qualifying marks are 40% for General/EWS candidates and 35% for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD candidates, as prescribed in the notification.

Final Merit: The final combined score is prepared using the Online Main Examination and Interview in the prescribed ratio of 80:20 respectively. Candidates must qualify both the Main Examination and Interview and rank sufficiently high in merit for provisional allotment.

IBPS SO Interview & Final Selection

🎤

1. Interview

Candidates who qualify the Online Main Examination and are shortlisted according to the applicable IBPS rules are called for the Interview. The Interview assesses the candidate's professional knowledge, suitability, communication skills, awareness and overall suitability for the Specialist Officer post.

📄

2. Document Verification

Candidates must produce the prescribed original documents and self-attested copies at the Interview / Document Verification stage. These documents are used to verify educational qualification, age, category, identity and other eligibility conditions.

⚖️

3. Main Exam & Interview Weightage

The relative weightage of the Online Main Examination and Interview is 80:20. The combined final score is prepared using the applicable Main Examination score and Interview score.

🏆

4. Final Merit

Candidates must qualify both the Online Main Examination and Interview. The final merit is prepared on the basis of the combined score, applicable qualifying conditions, vacancies and the recruitment rules prescribed by IBPS.

🏦

5. Provisional Allotment

Candidates who are sufficiently high in the final merit are considered for provisional allotment to participating banks according to merit, vacancies, preferences and the applicable IBPS rules.

📋

6. Joining & Appointment

After provisional allotment and completion of the required verification and other formalities, the allotted bank carries out the appointment and joining process according to its applicable rules and requirements.

Important Note: IBPS SO final selection is not based on the Preliminary Examination marks. The Preliminary Examination is used for shortlisting candidates for the Main Examination. Candidates must qualify the prescribed Main Examination and Interview requirements. The relative weightage of the Online Main Examination and Interview is 80:20 for preparation of the combined final score. Candidates should always follow the latest CRP-SPL notification for the exact qualifying criteria, merit and allotment procedure.

Selection Process

📝

1. Preliminary Examination

Candidates first appear for the Online Preliminary Examination. The pattern and subjects vary according to the Specialist Officer post. Candidates who qualify the prescribed requirements are shortlisted for the Main Examination.

📚

2. Main Examination

Shortlisted candidates appear for the Online Main Examination. The Main Examination primarily assesses Professional Knowledge according to the applied Specialist Officer post, along with the applicable tests prescribed by IBPS.

🎤

3. Interview

Candidates who qualify the Main Examination and meet the applicable shortlisting requirements are called for the Interview. The Interview assesses professional suitability, communication, awareness and overall suitability for the Specialist Officer post.

⚖️

4. Main Exam & Interview Weightage

The Online Main Examination and Interview are considered in the prescribed 80:20 ratio for the final combined score. Candidates must qualify the applicable requirements of both stages.

🏆

5. Final Merit

Final merit is prepared using the combined Main Examination and Interview scores according to the applicable IBPS rules, qualifying conditions, vacancies and other recruitment requirements.

🏦

6. Provisional Allotment

Candidates who are sufficiently high in the final merit are considered for provisional allotment to participating public sector banks according to merit, vacancies, preferences and the applicable IBPS rules.

📄

7. Document Verification

Selected candidates must produce the required original documents and supporting certificates for verification as prescribed by IBPS and the allotted bank.

🏢

8. Joining & Appointment

After successful completion of provisional allotment, document verification and other applicable formalities, candidates join the allotted participating public sector bank according to the bank's joining and appointment procedures.

Important Note: The Preliminary Examination is used for shortlisting candidates for the Main Examination. Final selection is based on the applicable Main Examination and Interview performance, with the prescribed 80:20 weightage. The exact selection, shortlisting, allotment, verification and joining procedure may vary according to the post and latest IBPS CRP-SPL notification.

Important Information

IBPS Specialist Officer (SO) recruitment is conducted through the Common Recruitment Process for Specialist Officers (CRP-SPL) by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) for participating public sector banks. Candidates should carefully check the latest official IBPS notification for the applied Specialist Officer post, including eligibility, age limit, educational and professional qualification, vacancies, examination pattern, selection process and other applicable conditions.

IBPS SO क्या है?

IBPS Specialist Officer (SO) एक राष्ट्रीय स्तर की बैंकिंग भर्ती परीक्षा है, जिसे Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) द्वारा भाग लेने वाले सार्वजनिक क्षेत्र के बैंकों में Specialist Officer पदों पर भर्ती के लिए आयोजित किया जाता है।

यह भर्ती Common Recruitment Process (CRP-SPL) के माध्यम से विभिन्न Specialist Officer पदों के लिए आयोजित की जाती है, जैसे IT Officer, Agricultural Field Officer, Rajbhasha Adhikari, Law Officer, HR/Personnel Officer और Marketing Officer, जैसा कि लागू IBPS अधिसूचना में निर्धारित किया जाता है।

IBPS SO उन उम्मीदवारों के लिए विशेष अवसर प्रदान करता है जिनके पास संबंधित क्षेत्र की विशेषज्ञ शैक्षणिक योग्यता है और जो भाग लेने वाले सार्वजनिक क्षेत्र के बैंकों में Specialist Officer के रूप में कार्य करना चाहते हैं। उम्मीदवारों का चयन नवीनतम IBPS CRP-SPL अधिसूचना में निर्धारित चरणों के अनुसार किया जाता है।

महत्वपूर्ण: भाग लेने वाले बैंक, रिक्तियां, पात्रता मानदंड, Specialist Officer पद, परीक्षा पैटर्न, चयन प्रक्रिया और अन्य शर्तें नवीनतम IBPS CRP-SPL अधिसूचना के अनुसार बदल सकती हैं। उम्मीदवारों को आवेदन करने से पहले हमेशा नवीनतम आधिकारिक IBPS अधिसूचना अवश्य देखनी चाहिए।

IBPS SO साक्षात्कार एवं अंतिम चयन

🎤

1. साक्षात्कार (Interview)

Online Main Examination में निर्धारित योग्यता प्राप्त करने और लागू IBPS नियमों के अनुसार shortlist होने वाले उम्मीदवारों को Interview के लिए बुलाया जाता है। Interview में उम्मीदवार के Professional Knowledge, पद के लिए suitability, communication skills, awareness और Specialist Officer पद के लिए overall suitability का आकलन किया जाता है।

📄

2. दस्तावेज़ सत्यापन

उम्मीदवारों को Interview / Document Verification के समय निर्धारित original documents और self-attested copies प्रस्तुत करनी होती हैं। इन दस्तावेज़ों के माध्यम से educational qualification, age, category, identity और अन्य eligibility conditions का सत्यापन किया जाता है।

⚖️

3. Main Exam एवं Interview का Weightage

Online Main Examination और Interview का relative weightage 80:20 होता है। Combined final score लागू Main Examination score और Interview score के आधार पर तैयार किया जाता है।

🏆

4. अंतिम मेरिट

उम्मीदवारों को Online Main Examination और Interview दोनों में निर्धारित योग्यता प्राप्त करनी होती है। Final merit combined score, लागू qualifying conditions, उपलब्ध vacancies और IBPS द्वारा निर्धारित recruitment rules के आधार पर तैयार की जाती है।

🏦

5. Provisional Allotment

Final merit में पर्याप्त उच्च स्थान प्राप्त करने वाले उम्मीदवारों को merit, vacancies, preferences और लागू IBPS rules के अनुसार participating banks में provisional allotment के लिए consider किया जाता है।

📋

6. Joining एवं Appointment

Provisional allotment और आवश्यक verification तथा अन्य formalities पूरी होने के बाद allotted bank अपने लागू नियमों और requirements के अनुसार appointment एवं joining की प्रक्रिया पूरी करता है।

महत्वपूर्ण नोट: IBPS SO का final selection Preliminary Examination के marks के आधार पर नहीं होता। Preliminary Examination का उपयोग Main Examination के लिए candidates को shortlist करने के लिए किया जाता है। उम्मीदवारों को निर्धारित Main Examination और Interview की qualifying requirements पूरी करनी होती हैं। Online Main Examination और Interview का relative weightage final combined score तैयार करने के लिए 80:20 होता है। उम्मीदवारों को exact qualifying criteria, merit और allotment procedure के लिए हमेशा नवीनतम CRP-SPL notification को देखना चाहिए।

चयन प्रक्रिया

📝

1. प्रारंभिक परीक्षा

उम्मीदवार सबसे पहले ऑनलाइन प्रारंभिक परीक्षा में शामिल होते हैं। परीक्षा का पैटर्न और विषय Specialist Officer के पद के अनुसार अलग-अलग हो सकते हैं। निर्धारित आवश्यकताओं को पूरा करने वाले उम्मीदवारों को मुख्य परीक्षा के लिए शॉर्टलिस्ट किया जाता है।

📚

2. मुख्य परीक्षा

शॉर्टलिस्ट किए गए उम्मीदवार ऑनलाइन मुख्य परीक्षा में शामिल होते हैं। मुख्य परीक्षा में मुख्य रूप से आवेदन किए गए Specialist Officer पद के अनुसार Professional Knowledge का आकलन किया जाता है, साथ ही IBPS द्वारा निर्धारित लागू Tests भी शामिल हो सकते हैं।

🎤

3. साक्षात्कार

मुख्य परीक्षा में सफल होने वाले और लागू Shortlisting Requirements को पूरा करने वाले उम्मीदवारों को Interview के लिए बुलाया जाता है। Interview में Professional Suitability, Communication, Awareness और Specialist Officer पद के लिए Overall Suitability का आकलन किया जाता है।

⚖️

4. मुख्य परीक्षा और साक्षात्कार का वेटेज

Online Main Examination और Interview को अंतिम संयुक्त स्कोर के लिए निर्धारित 80:20 अनुपात में माना जाता है। उम्मीदवारों को दोनों चरणों की लागू आवश्यकताओं को पूरा करना होता है।

🏆

5. अंतिम मेरिट

Final Merit, Main Examination और Interview में प्राप्त संयुक्त अंकों के आधार पर लागू IBPS नियमों, Qualifying Conditions, उपलब्ध Vacancies और अन्य Recruitment Requirements के अनुसार तैयार की जाती है।

🏦

6. अनंतिम आवंटन

Final Merit में पर्याप्त उच्च स्थान प्राप्त करने वाले उम्मीदवारों को Merit, Vacancies, Preferences और लागू IBPS Rules के अनुसार Participating Public Sector Banks में Provisional Allotment के लिए विचार किया जाता है।

📄

7. दस्तावेज़ सत्यापन

चयनित उम्मीदवारों को IBPS और आवंटित बैंक द्वारा निर्धारित Verification के लिए आवश्यक Original Documents और Supporting Certificates प्रस्तुत करने होते हैं।

🏢

8. जॉइनिंग और नियुक्ति

Provisional Allotment, Document Verification और अन्य लागू Formalities सफलतापूर्वक पूरी होने के बाद उम्मीदवार संबंधित Participating Public Sector Bank में बैंक की Joining और Appointment Procedures के अनुसार कार्यभार ग्रहण करते हैं।

महत्वपूर्ण नोट: प्रारंभिक परीक्षा का उपयोग उम्मीदवारों को मुख्य परीक्षा के लिए Shortlist करने के लिए किया जाता है। अंतिम चयन लागू Main Examination और Interview में प्रदर्शन के आधार पर निर्धारित 80:20 Weightage के अनुसार किया जाता है। चयन, Shortlisting, Allotment, Verification और Joining की सटीक प्रक्रिया पद और नवीनतम IBPS CRP-SPL Notification के अनुसार अलग हो सकती है।

महत्वपूर्ण जानकारी

IBPS Specialist Officer (SO) भर्ती, Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) द्वारा Participating Public Sector Banks के लिए Common Recruitment Process for Specialist Officers (CRP-SPL) के माध्यम से आयोजित की जाती है। उम्मीदवारों को आवेदन किए गए Specialist Officer Post के लिए नवीनतम Official IBPS Notification को ध्यानपूर्वक देखना चाहिए, जिसमें Eligibility, Age Limit, Educational और Professional Qualification, Vacancies, Examination Pattern, Selection Process और अन्य लागू Conditions शामिल होती हैं।

Preliminary Examination का आयोजन उम्मीदवारों को Main Examination के लिए Shortlist करने के उद्देश्य से किया जाता है। Preliminary Examination का Pattern Specialist Officer Post के अनुसार अलग-अलग हो सकता है। लागू Preliminary Examination Requirements को पूरा करने वाले उम्मीदवारों को Main Examination के लिए Shortlist किया जाता है, जिसमें Post के अनुसार निर्धारित Professional Knowledge Test और अन्य लागू Sections शामिल होते हैं।

Main Examination में सफल होने वाले और लागू Shortlisting Requirements को पूरा करने वाले उम्मीदवारों को Interview के लिए बुलाया जाता है। Final Selection Score निर्धारित Main Examination और Interview Weightage के आधार पर तैयार किया जाता है। उम्मीदवारों को Provisional Allotment और Joining से पहले Document Verification तथा अन्य आवश्यक Formalities भी पूरी करनी होती हैं।

Participating Banks, Vacancies, Post-Wise Eligibility, Examination Dates, Subjects, Selection Rules, Interview Requirements और अन्य Recruitment Conditions को Official IBPS Notifications या Corrigenda के माध्यम से बदला या Update किया जा सकता है। इसलिए उम्मीदवारों को आवेदन करने या कोई महत्वपूर्ण निर्णय लेने से पहले हमेशा Official IBPS Website पर नवीनतम जानकारी Verify करनी चाहिए।
