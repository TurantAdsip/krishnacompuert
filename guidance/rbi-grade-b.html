What is RBI Grade B?

The RBI Grade B examination is conducted by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) for the recruitment of Grade B Officers in various streams, primarily General, DEPR and DSIM, as prescribed in the latest official recruitment notification.

RBI Grade B is one of the most prestigious and competitive examinations in the banking and financial sector. Selected Grade B Officers work in different functional areas of the Reserve Bank of India and may be involved in monetary policy, banking regulation, financial supervision, economic analysis, research, administration and other responsibilities according to their department and posting.

The recruitment process generally includes Phase-I, Phase-II and Interview for the General stream, while the examination structure for DEPR and DSIM is prescribed separately according to the applicable RBI notification. Candidates should carefully check the latest official RBI Grade B notification for eligibility, vacancies, syllabus, examination pattern, selection process and other applicable conditions.

RBI Grade B Posts & Streams

🏦

Grade B Officer — General (DR)

RBI Grade B General is the primary recruitment stream for officers in the Reserve Bank of India. Officers may work in areas such as banking regulation, financial supervision, monetary policy, economic and financial analysis, currency management, administration and other functions assigned by RBI.

📊

Grade B Officer — DEPR

The Department of Economic and Policy Research (DEPR) stream is designed for candidates with the prescribed economics-related academic qualifications. Officers may contribute to economic research, policy analysis, data analysis and research-related activities of the Reserve Bank.

📈

Grade B Officer — DSIM

The Department of Statistics and Information Management (DSIM) stream is intended for candidates with the prescribed statistics, mathematics, data science or related qualifications. Officers may work on statistical analysis, data management, research and information systems.

💼

Officer-Level Responsibility

RBI Grade B Officers work in an officer-level role and may be assigned responsibilities involving policy implementation, regulatory work, research, supervision, analysis, administration and other functions depending on their department and posting.

📚

Career Growth

RBI provides opportunities for career progression through promotions and departmental responsibilities. The career path and nature of work may vary according to the officer's stream, department, performance, experience and organisational requirements.

🏛️

Work in the Reserve Bank of India

Grade B Officers may be posted at RBI offices and work with different departments of the central bank. Their responsibilities can involve India's banking and financial system, economic policy, regulation, supervision, research and other important central banking functions.

Important Note: RBI Grade B vacancies, streams, departments, post names, eligibility requirements and job responsibilities may vary according to the latest RBI recruitment notification. Candidates should always check the official RBI notification for the applicable recruitment year.

RBI Grade B Exam Pattern

📝

Phase-I Examination

For the General stream, the RBI Grade B Phase-I Examination is an objective-type online examination consisting of General Awareness, English Language, Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning. Candidates must qualify the prescribed sectional and overall requirements to proceed to Phase-II.

⏱️

Phase-I Time Management

Phase-I is conducted within the time limits prescribed by RBI, with applicable sectional timing. Candidates should manage their time carefully and maintain a balance between speed and accuracy while attempting the objective questions.

📚

Phase-II Examination

For the General stream, Phase-II generally consists of papers covering Economic & Social Issues, English (Writing Skills) and Finance & Management. The exact paper structure, marks and duration are prescribed in the latest RBI notification.

📊

Economic & Social Issues

This paper covers important areas of Indian economy, economic policies, growth and development, social issues, demographic trends, globalization, sustainable development and other topics prescribed by RBI.

💰

Finance & Management

This paper covers financial systems, financial markets, banking, risk management, corporate finance, accounting, management concepts, organisational behaviour and other finance and management topics included in the RBI Grade B syllabus.

✍️

English — Writing Skills

The English paper assesses candidates' ability to express ideas clearly and effectively in written English through descriptive writing tasks. Candidates should develop essay writing, comprehension, précis and other relevant writing skills according to the prescribed syllabus.

🎤

Interview

Candidates who qualify the applicable Phase-II requirements are shortlisted for the Interview stage according to RBI's prescribed criteria. The Interview assesses personality, communication, awareness and overall suitability for the Grade B Officer role.

❌

Negative Marking

Negative marking is applicable to objective-type questions as prescribed by RBI. Candidates should carefully check the latest notification for the exact marking scheme and avoid unnecessary guessing.

🏆

Final Selection

Final selection is made on the basis of performance in the applicable Phase-II papers and Interview, subject to the prescribed eligibility, qualifying requirements and merit criteria. Phase-I is generally used for shortlisting candidates for Phase-II.

📈

DEPR & DSIM Examination

The examination pattern for the DEPR and DSIM streams is different from the General stream and is based on the specialised subjects prescribed for those streams. Candidates should refer to the latest RBI notification for the exact paper structure and selection process.

Important Note: RBI Grade B examination pattern, number of questions, marks, duration, sectional requirements, syllabus, negative marking, Phase-II papers and Interview criteria may be revised by RBI. Candidates should always follow the latest official RBI Grade B notification for the applicable recruitment cycle.

RBI Grade B — Selection Process

📝

1. Phase-I Examination

Candidates first appear for the RBI Grade B Phase-I examination. For the General cadre, the Phase-I examination is an objective online examination covering General Awareness, Reasoning, English Language, Quantitative Aptitude and related areas as prescribed by RBI. Candidates who meet the applicable sectional and aggregate requirements are shortlisted for Phase-II.

📚

2. Phase-II Examination

Candidates shortlisted from Phase-I appear for the Phase-II examination. For Grade B (DR)-General, Phase-II consists of Economic and Social Issues, English (Writing Skills) and General Finance and Management. The examination may include objective and descriptive components according to the prescribed RBI examination scheme.

🎯

3. Phase-II Shortlisting

Candidates who qualify the prescribed Phase-II sectional and aggregate requirements are shortlisted for the Interview. RBI determines the applicable qualifying standards and shortlisting criteria according to the recruitment notification and number of vacancies.

🎤

4. Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates appear for the Personal Interview. The Interview assesses the candidate's personality, communication skills, subject knowledge, economic and financial awareness, current affairs, suitability for the role and overall understanding of relevant issues.

🏦

5. RBI & Banking Awareness

Candidates should prepare RBI functions, monetary policy, banking and financial systems, economic developments, financial regulations, government policies, current economic issues and important national and international developments before the Interview.

📊

6. Final Merit

For the Grade B recruitment, the final selection is based on the candidate's performance in Phase-II and the Interview, according to the weightage and qualifying requirements prescribed in the latest RBI recruitment notification. Phase-I is used for shortlisting candidates for Phase-II.

📄

7. Document Verification

Shortlisted candidates must produce original certificates and supporting documents for verification. These may include educational qualification documents, identity proof, category certificates and other documents required by RBI.

🏥

8. Medical Examination

Candidates selected for appointment must satisfy the applicable medical fitness requirements prescribed by RBI. Medical examination and related formalities are completed according to the Bank's recruitment rules.

🏆

9. Final Selection & Appointment

Candidates who successfully complete the examination, Interview, document verification, medical requirements and other applicable formalities are considered for appointment as Officers in Grade B according to RBI's recruitment and joining procedures.

Important Note: The RBI Grade B selection process, examination structure, qualifying standards, Interview requirements, weightage and other recruitment conditions may be revised by the Reserve Bank of India. Candidates should always follow the latest official RBI Grade B recruitment notification for the applicable recruitment cycle.

The number of vacancies, examination dates, syllabus, examination pattern, application fee, qualifying standards, selection procedure and other recruitment conditions may be changed or updated through official RBI notifications. RBI Grade B — चयन प्रक्रिया

📝

1. Phase-I परीक्षा

उम्मीदवार सबसे पहले RBI Grade B Phase-I परीक्षा में शामिल होते हैं। General cadre के लिए Phase-I एक Objective Online Examination होती है, जिसमें RBI द्वारा निर्धारित General Awareness, Reasoning, English Language, Quantitative Aptitude तथा अन्य संबंधित क्षेत्र शामिल होते हैं। निर्धारित sectional और aggregate requirements को पूरा करने वाले उम्मीदवारों को Phase-II के लिए shortlist किया जाता है।

📚

2. Phase-II परीक्षा

Phase-I से shortlisted उम्मीदवार Phase-II परीक्षा में शामिल होते हैं। Grade B (DR)-General के लिए Phase-II में Economic and Social Issues, English (Writing Skills) तथा General Finance and Management शामिल होते हैं। परीक्षा में RBI द्वारा निर्धारित examination scheme के अनुसार objective और descriptive components हो सकते हैं।

🎯

3. Phase-II Shortlisting

निर्धारित Phase-II sectional और aggregate requirements को पूरा करने वाले उम्मीदवारों को Interview के लिए shortlist किया जाता है। RBI recruitment notification तथा vacancies की संख्या के अनुसार applicable qualifying standards और shortlisting criteria निर्धारित करता है।

🎤

4. Personal Interview

Shortlisted उम्मीदवार Personal Interview में शामिल होते हैं। Interview में उम्मीदवार की personality, communication skills, subject knowledge, economic and financial awareness, current affairs, पद के लिए suitability तथा relevant issues की overall understanding का आकलन किया जाता है।

🏦

5. RBI & Banking Awareness

Interview से पहले उम्मीदवारों को RBI के functions, monetary policy, banking and financial systems, economic developments, financial regulations, government policies, current economic issues तथा महत्वपूर्ण national और international developments की तैयारी करनी चाहिए।

📊

6. Final Merit

Grade B recruitment में final selection Phase-II और Interview में उम्मीदवार के performance के आधार पर किया जाता है। इसके लिए latest RBI recruitment notification में निर्धारित weightage और qualifying requirements लागू होते हैं। Phase-I का उपयोग सामान्यतः Phase-II के लिए candidates को shortlist करने के लिए किया जाता है।

📄

7. Document Verification

Shortlisted उम्मीदवारों को verification के लिए original certificates और supporting documents प्रस्तुत करने होते हैं। इनमें educational qualification documents, identity proof, category certificates तथा RBI द्वारा मांगे गए अन्य आवश्यक documents शामिल हो सकते हैं।

🏥

8. Medical Examination

Appointment के लिए selected candidates को RBI द्वारा निर्धारित applicable medical fitness requirements को पूरा करना होता है। Medical examination और संबंधित formalities Bank के recruitment rules के अनुसार पूरी की जाती हैं।

🏆

9. Final Selection & Appointment

जो उम्मीदवार examination, Interview, document verification, medical requirements तथा अन्य applicable formalities को सफलतापूर्वक पूरा करते हैं, उन्हें RBI की recruitment और joining procedures के अनुसार Grade B Officer के रूप में appointment के लिए consider किया जाता है।

महत्वपूर्ण नोट: RBI Grade B की selection process, examination structure, qualifying standards, Interview requirements, weightage तथा अन्य recruitment conditions को Reserve Bank of India द्वारा संशोधित किया जा सकता है। उम्मीदवारों को हमेशा संबंधित recruitment cycle की latest official RBI Grade B recruitment notification को ही अंतिम और विश्वसनीय स्रोत मानना चाहिए।

Grade B (DR) — General के लिए selection process में सामान्यतः Phase-I, Phase-II और Interview शामिल होते हैं। Phase-I का मुख्य उद्देश्य candidates को Phase-II के लिए shortlist करना होता है, जबकि prescribed Phase-II requirements को पूरा करने वाले candidates को applicable recruitment rules के अनुसार Interview के लिए shortlist किया जाता है।

General cadre के लिए Phase-II examination में latest RBI notification के अनुसार Economic and Social Issues, English (Writing Skills) तथा Finance & Management जैसे विषय शामिल होते हैं। Candidates को objective और descriptive दोनों components की अच्छी तैयारी करनी चाहिए तथा economic, financial और banking concepts की मजबूत समझ विकसित करनी चाहिए।

Final selection applicable RBI recruitment rules के अनुसार किया जाता है, जिसमें Phase-II और Interview में performance, prescribed qualifying standards, merit, vacancies तथा अन्य eligibility conditions शामिल हो सकती हैं। Appointment से पहले candidates को document verification, medical examination तथा अन्य आवश्यक formalities भी पूरी करनी पड़ सकती हैं।

Vacancies की संख्या, examination dates, syllabus, examination pattern, application fee, qualifying standards, selection procedure तथा अन्य recruitment conditions को official RBI notifications के माध्यम से बदला या update किया जा सकता है। इसलिए candidates को आवेदन करने या कोई महत्वपूर्ण निर्णय लेने से पहले official RBI Opportunities portal और RBI website पर latest information अवश्य verify करनी चाहिए।
