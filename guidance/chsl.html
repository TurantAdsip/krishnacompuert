Complete SSC CHSL guidance — eligibility, posts, exam pattern, subjects, books, selection process and preparation strategy.

What is SSC CHSL?

The Staff Selection Commission Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination is conducted by the Staff Selection Commission for recruitment to various Group C posts in Ministries, Departments, offices and organisations of the Government of India.

SSC CHSL is generally conducted for posts such as Lower Divisional Clerk/Junior Secretariat Assistant and Data Entry Operator, subject to the posts and vacancies notified in the relevant examination year.

Major Posts Through SSC CHSL

01

Lower Divisional Clerk

LDC posts in various Central Government ministries, departments and offices as notified by SSC.

02

Junior Secretariat Assistant

JSA posts in participating government offices and departments according to the notified vacancies.

03

Data Entry Operator

DEO posts involving data-entry and office-related responsibilities, subject to notified vacancies and requirements.

04

Data Entry Operator Grade 'A'

Specific DEO Grade 'A' posts where notified under the applicable SSC CHSL recruitment.

The exact posts, departments, vacancies, pay levels and post-specific requirements can change between examination years. Always check the latest SSC CHSL notification.

Eligibility

Requirement General Information
Educational Qualification For most SSC CHSL posts, a candidate must have passed the 12th Standard or an equivalent examination from a recognised Board or University. Certain posts may have additional subject requirements.
Educational Stream For most posts, candidates from any recognised stream may be eligible. Certain DEO posts can require Science with Mathematics at the specified level.
Age Limit The age limit and cut-off date are specified in the latest SSC CHSL notification.
Nationality Nationality/citizenship conditions are specified in the applicable SSC rules and notification.
Special Qualification Some posts may have specific educational or skill requirements. Check the latest notification for post-wise eligibility.

Eligibility conditions can vary by post and examination year. Always verify the latest SSC CHSL notification before applying.

SSC periodically revises examination schemes. The latest official SSC CHSL notification should be treated as the final authority for the current pattern, modules, marks and qualifying tests.

Tier-I Subjects

Subject Important Areas
English Language Vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, comprehension, error detection, synonyms, antonyms and usage.
General Intelligence Analogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, logical reasoning, problem solving and non-verbal reasoning.
Quantitative Aptitude Number system, arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry and data interpretation.
General Awareness History, Geography, Polity, Economy, Science, Current Affairs and general knowledge.

Tier-II Subjects

Subject Important Areas
Mathematical Abilities Number system, arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration, trigonometry, statistics and related quantitative topics.
Reasoning and General Intelligence Verbal and non-verbal reasoning, analogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, logical reasoning and problem solving.
English Language and Comprehension Vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, comprehension, error detection, synonyms, antonyms and practical English usage.
General Awareness Current events, history, geography, polity, economy, science and general knowledge.
Computer Knowledge Basic computer concepts, hardware, software, operating systems, internet, networking, cybersecurity and common applications.
Skill Test / Typing Test Skill Test or Typing Test is conducted as applicable to the post under the latest SSC CHSL examination scheme.

Selection Process

01

Tier-I

Computer Based Examination covering English Language, General Intelligence, Quantitative Aptitude and General Awareness.

02

Tier-II

Computer Based Examination covering the subjects and modules prescribed under the latest SSC CHSL examination scheme.

03

Skill / Typing Test

Skill Test or Typing Test is conducted for the posts where it is applicable according to the latest examination scheme.

04

Document Verification

Educational, identity, category and other required documents are verified according to SSC instructions.

05

Post Preference

Post and department preferences are considered according to the procedure and instructions applicable to the examination year.

06

Final Allocation

Final allocation is made according to merit, preferences, vacancies, eligibility and applicable recruitment rules.

SSC CHSL क्या है?

Staff Selection Commission Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination, Staff Selection Commission द्वारा भारत सरकार के विभिन्न Ministries, Departments, offices और organisations में विभिन्न Group C posts पर भर्ती के लिए आयोजित की जाती है।

SSC CHSL सामान्यतः Lower Divisional Clerk/Junior Secretariat Assistant और Data Entry Operator जैसी posts के लिए आयोजित की जाती है। Posts और vacancies संबंधित examination year में जारी notification के अनुसार होती हैं।
